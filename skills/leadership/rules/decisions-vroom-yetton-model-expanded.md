Expanded guidance on choosing decision-making approaches:

**Decision quality factors**:
- Is there a demonstrably better solution? (If no, decision quality is less important)
- Do I have sufficient information to make a good decision?
- Is problem structured or unstructured?

**Acceptance factors**:
- Do people need to accept the decision for implementation?
- Will people accept my decision? (Depends on trust, legitimacy, reasoning)
- Are people likely to disagree with my preferred solution?

**Process**:

1. **If high quality AND high acceptance needed** → Participative or consensus. Involve others to get buy-in and improve quality.

2. **If high quality needed BUT acceptance uncertain** → Consultative. Gather input, then decide.

3. **If high acceptance needed BUT quality straightforward** → Participative. Let people have input to build buy-in.

4. **If time is critical** → Autocratic. Make fast decision, then explain reasoning to build acceptance retroactively.

5. **If low stakes** → Either works. Use participative for development opportunity.
