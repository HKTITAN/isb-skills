**Expand the pie before claiming your slice**:

Traditional negotiation is dividing fixed pie (distributive). Creative problem-solving expands the pie (integrative):
- Bundle issues: What I care about most may be cheap for you to give; find these trades
- Differences in time: Phased payments, future contingencies
- Differences in risk tolerance: Risk premium for who bears risk
- Differences in constraints: Work around each other's constraints (timing, currency, structure)

**Example**: Seller wants $10M for business. Buyer has $6M cash but strong cash flow. Solution: $6M cash now, $4M earnout over 3 years based on performance. Seller gets upside if business thrives; buyer gets discount for risk.

**Claiming value**: Once pie is expanded, negotiate the split. Tactics:
- Make first offer (anchoring)
- Justify opening offer thoroughly
- Make smaller concessions as negotiation progresses
- Trade concessions explicitly ("if you move on price, I can move on timing")
