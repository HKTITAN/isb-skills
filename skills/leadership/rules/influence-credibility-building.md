Three components of credibility:

1. **Competence**: Knowledge, expertise, track record; demonstrated ability
2. **Trustworthiness**: Reliability, integrity, consistency; predictable behavior
3. **Goodwill**: Genuine interest in others' welfare, not just self-serving

Build credibility through: Transparent reasoning, acknowledging limitations, following through on commitments, aligning words with actions, considering others' interests.

Credibility is earned slowly and lost quickly; maintain it through consistency and integrity.
