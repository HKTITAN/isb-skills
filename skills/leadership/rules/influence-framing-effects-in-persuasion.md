Same information presented differently influences decisions:

- **Gain frame** vs. **loss frame**: "This will save us $1M" vs. "Not doing this will cost us $1M"
  - Gain frame: Risk-averse (people conservatively prefer sure gains)
  - Loss frame: Risk-seeking (people will take chances to avoid losses)

- **Broad vs. narrow frame**: Considering long-term strategy vs. short-term metrics
  - Broad: More aligned with organizational health
  - Narrow: Easier to measure but may miss bigger picture

**Ethical persuasion** presents information honestly in the frame most appropriate for the decision, not the frame that serves your interests.
