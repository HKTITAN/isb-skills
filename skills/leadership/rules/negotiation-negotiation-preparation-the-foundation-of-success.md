**Three foundations**:

1. **Interests vs. Positions**: Your position is what you say you want (e.g., "I want $500K"). Your interests are why you want it (flexibility in payment terms, risk mitigation, growth opportunity). Understanding underlying interests opens creative solutions. Probe: "Why is that important to you?"

2. **Information gathering**:
   - About other party: Financial condition, constraints, decision-making process, priorities
   - About market: Standards, comparable deals, alternatives available to them
   - About yourself: Your real BATNA, your flexibility, your true interests

3. **Strategy formulation**:
   - Define your target (ideal outcome)
   - Define your reservation price (walk-away)
   - Identify key issues (must-have, nice-to-have, trade-able)
   - Plan opening offer (should be ambitious but defensible)
   - Plan concession strategy (what will you give up, in what order)
   - Identify potential creative solutions (expanding pie before claiming it)

**Planning principle**: Adequate preparation determines 80% of negotiation success.
