CEO must balance competing stakeholder interests. Stakeholder management matrices help prioritize attention:

**Stakeholder Mapping** (two dimensions):
1. **Power**: How much influence do they have over your success?
2. **Interest**: How much do they care about your organization?

This creates four quadrants:
- **High Power, High Interest** (Manage closely): Board, major customers, key employees, major investors. Requires active engagement, transparent communication, alignment.
- **High Power, Low Interest** (Keep satisfied): Regulators, passive investors, potential competitors. Maintain good relationships but less intensive management.
- **Low Power, High Interest** (Keep informed): Employees, customers, community. Important for morale and word-of-mouth; regular communication sufficient.
- **Low Power, Low Interest** (Monitor): Occasional communication sufficient.

**Key stakeholders and relationship management**:

**Shareholders/Investors**: Want returns. Relationship requires:
- Honest communication about progress, risks, strategy
- Regular updates on financial performance
- Acknowledgment of their interests in major decisions
- Transparency about challenges before they become crises

**Employees**: Want meaningful work, growth, stability, fair treatment. Relationship requires:
- Transparent communication about strategy and challenges
- Consistency in decisions and values
- Investment in their development
- Fair compensation and recognition
- Psychological safety to contribute

**Customers**: Want reliable products, good service, fair value. Relationship requires:
- Deep understanding of their business and needs
- Delivery on promises consistently
- Responsiveness to issues
- Regular communication and relationship investment
- Anticipating needs, not just reacting

**Regulators/Government**: Want compliance, ethical behavior, positive impact. Relationship requires:
- Proactive engagement (not waiting for problems)
- Transparent reporting and communication
- Understanding regulatory environment and constraints
- Participating in industry dialogue

**Board/Governance**: Want oversight, risk management, compliance, strategy success. Relationship requires:
- Honest reporting; no surprises
- Strategic partnership (not adversarial)
- Regular communication
- Clear accountability for execution

**Community/Society**: Want ethical behavior, positive contribution, transparency. Relationship requires:
- Ethical business practices
- Community engagement and contribution
- Environmental responsibility
- Transparency on impact

**Competing interests**: Stakeholders often want conflicting things (customers want lower prices; shareholders want higher margins). CEO role is managing trade-offs explicitly, not pretending conflicts don't exist. Good stakeholder management requires:
1. Understanding each stakeholder's perspective and constraints
2. Being explicit about trade-offs being made
3. Transparency about reasoning
4. Consistency over time (predictable even if unpopular)
