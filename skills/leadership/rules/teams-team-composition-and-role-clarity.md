**Belbin Team Roles**: Teams function best with diverse functional roles:
- Plant (innovator, creative)
- Resource Investigator (networker, enthusiastic)
- Coordinator (organizer, confident)
- Monitor-Evaluator (critical, analytical)
- Teamworker (cooperative, diplomatic)
- Implementer (disciplined, reliable)
- Completer-Finisher (detail-focused, perfectionist)
- Specialist (expert, focused)

High-performing teams have coverage across roles. Imbalance (all plants, no implementers) creates problems.

**Role clarity**: Each member should understand: What am I accountable for? What are my boundaries? How does my role connect to others? Ambiguous roles create conflict and inefficiency.
