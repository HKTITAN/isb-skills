**How to lead during crisis**:

**Phase 1: Immediate Response (First 24-48 hours)**
- Assess facts: What happened? Scope? Root cause?
- Secure situation: Prevent escalation, protect people/assets
- Communicate transparently: Internal first, then external; avoid speculation
- Establish command structure: Clear decision authority

**Phase 2: Response (Days 1-30)**
- Conduct thorough investigation
- Develop remediation plan
- Communicate regularly; show progress
- Protect key stakeholders from surprises
- Engage legal/PR counsel
- Document decisions (audit trail)

**Phase 3: Recovery (Month 2 onward)**
- Implement prevention measures
- Restore normal operations
- Rebuild trust with stakeholders
- Shift narrative from crisis to forward

**Crisis and character**: How you respond defines your leadership. Transparency, decisiveness, and accountability earn respect even if outcomes are painful.
