Herzberg identified two separate dimensions affecting motivation:

**Hygiene Factors** (Dissatisfiers - their absence causes problems):
- Compensation and benefits
- Working conditions
- Job security
- Organizational policies
- Supervision quality
- Relationships with colleagues

**Improving hygiene factors eliminates dissatisfaction but doesn't create satisfaction.** You need adequate salary to function, but doubling salary doesn't double motivation.

**Motivators** (Satisfiers - their presence creates engagement):
- Achievement and accomplishment
- Recognition and praise
- Interesting work
- Responsibility and autonomy
- Growth and advancement
- Purpose and meaning

**Key insight**: Leaders must first address hygiene factors (don't underpay, don't create awful conditions), then focus on motivators. Many organizations stay stuck addressing hygiene while wondering why engagement is low.
