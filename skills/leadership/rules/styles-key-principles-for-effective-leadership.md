**1. Leadership is fundamentally about influence, not authority.**
Authority gets people to comply; influence gets them to commit. Build influence through competence, trust, and alignment.

**2. Self-awareness precedes impact.**
You can't manage what you don't understand. Seek feedback relentlessly. Know your triggers, biases, and blind spots.

**3. Motivation is internal, not external.**
Money and title are hygiene factors, not motivators. Real motivation comes from autonomy, competence, and relatedness. Design work around these.

**4. Culture beats strategy.**
Culture is how things get done when no one is watching. Strategy execution fails if culture doesn't support it. Change culture through hiring, promotion, what you measure, and what you model.

**5. Decision-making is a process, not an event.**
Good decisions come from clear information, diverse perspectives, and structured thinking. Biases distort all decisions; systems and teams correct for them better than individual willpower.

**6. Relationships matter more than you think.**
Organizational effectiveness depends on trust. Trust comes from consistent behavior, transparency, follow-through, and genuine care for others' welfare.

**7. Simplicity is power.**
Complex organizations, strategies, and messages fail. Clarity about what matters most, why it matters, and how to achieve it drives execution. Ruthlessly prioritize.

**8. Integrity is non-negotiable.**
Short-term advantage from cutting corners is not worth long-term cost to reputation, culture, and relationships. Lead by values, not just results.

**9. Development is the highest-leverage leadership activity.**
The best leaders invest in developing others. Your impact multiplies through their growth. Coaching, feedback, and stretch assignments compound over time.

**10. Leadership is learned.**
You're not born a great leader. Emotional intelligence, influence, strategic thinking—all can be developed. Commit to continuous learning and improvement.
