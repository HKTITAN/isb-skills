**Regulatory Requirements**:

**Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX)** (United States, 2002):

- Response to accounting scandals (Enron, WorldCom)
- Key requirements:
  - Audit committee must have financial expert
  - CEO/CFO certify financial statements
  - Internal control assessments
  - Audit independence requirements
  - Limits on non-audit services from auditors
- Impact: Increased compliance costs, more formal governance processes

**Dodd-Frank Act** (United States, financial institutions):

- Response to 2008 financial crisis
- Key requirements:
  - Risk oversight by board
  - Executive compensation disclosure
  - Clawback provisions for misconduct
  - Whistle-blower protections
- Impact: Changed executive compensation and risk management practices

**GDPR and Data Privacy**: European and global impact on governance around data protection, privacy, customer rights.
