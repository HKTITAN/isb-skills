title: "BATNA: Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement"
impact: CRITICAL

**Definition**: Your best course of action if negotiation fails. Your walk-away point.

**Why BATNA matters**: Stronger BATNA = stronger negotiating position. Negotiating without clear BATNA leads to accepting bad deals or overestimating your leverage.

**How to identify your BATNA**:

1. List all possible actions if deal doesn't happen
2. Develop each option as much as feasible
3. Select best alternative
4. Assign realistic value to it (monetary or otherwise)

**Strengthening your BATNA**: Your negotiating power comes from having options. Invest in developing them:
- Build relationships with alternative suppliers/partners
- Develop capabilities that reduce dependence on deal
- Create timeline flexibility so you're not desperate

**Using BATNA effectively**:
- Know it precisely but don't disclose it
- Use it to set your reservation price (walk-away point)
- Reference it indirectly to increase credibility ("I have other options")
- Improve your BATNA if current deal terms approach or exceed it

**Recognizing weak BATNA**: If you have no walk-away point, you have no real negotiating power. This creates take-it-or-leave-it situations favoring other party.
