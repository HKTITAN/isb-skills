**Vision**: Compelling picture of the desired future state that guides strategy and decisions.

Characteristics of powerful vision:
- Specific enough to guide (not vague platitude)
- Bold but credible
- Forward-looking (not just bigger version of today)
- Memorable and inspiring
- Aligned with organizational capabilities

**Strategy**: The pathway to realize the vision through competitive positioning, resource allocation, and execution.

Strategy requires:
- **Clarity**: What business are we in? What customers do we serve? What problems do we solve?
- **Differentiation**: Why would customers choose us over alternatives? What's our competitive advantage?
- **Resource focus**: Where will we concentrate resources? Where will we say no?
- **Execution**: How will we operationalize strategy? What capabilities do we need?
