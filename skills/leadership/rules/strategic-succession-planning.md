**Why succession matters**:
- Sudden CEO departure can destabilize company
- Weak succession creates leadership vacuum
- Strong bench increases resilience and optionality

**Succession process**:
1. **Identify high-potential leaders**: Who could be CEO in 5-10 years? Look for strategic thinking, people development, execution track record
2. **Develop them**: Stretch assignments (run new businesses, P&L responsibility), board exposure, mentor relationships, exposure to CEO thinking
3. **Evaluate readiness**: Can they do it? Do they want it? Are they culturally aligned?
4. **Decide**: Internal vs. external. Internal preserves culture continuity; external brings fresh perspective
5. **Communicate**: Prepare successor for potential role; manage expectations of disappointed others; maintain organizational stability

**Succession approaches**:
- **Grooming model**: Identify successor 2-3 years ahead; co-lead transition
- **Open competition**: Multiple candidates develop in parallel; board chooses
- **External search**: Brings outside perspective; signals change in direction
- **Interim CEO**: Non-traditional choice; works if credible leader able to stabilize

**Common mistakes**:
- **Waiting too long**: Plan while you're secure, not when you're leaving. External pressures force bad decisions.
- **Choosing a clone**: You need fresh perspective, not an extension of yourself
- **Ignoring culture fit**: Brilliant CEO from different industry/culture often fails
- **Not preparing organization**: Succession fails if organization doesn't believe successor capable
- **Weak bench**: If no internal options, external search is expensive and disruptive
- **Grandstanding departing CEO**: Hanging around after successor arrives undermines new leader's authority
- **Ignoring board**: Board has ultimate say on succession; work collaboratively

**Post-succession**: 
- Departing CEO needs clean exit (either leaves or has clearly defined advisory role)
- New CEO needs space to make mark without constantly compared to predecessor
- Organization needs to support transition; this isn't automatic
