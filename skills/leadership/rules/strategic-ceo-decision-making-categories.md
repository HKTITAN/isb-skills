**Directional decisions** (highest impact, highest stakes):
- Reshape strategy: new markets, new products, business model pivots, acquisitions
- Process: Gather diverse perspectives, challenge assumptions, test critical assumptions
- Frequency: Quarterly or less; can't easily reverse

**Operational decisions** (medium impact, moderate stakes):
- Optimize existing strategy: marketing campaigns, pricing, team restructuring
- Process: Delegate to functional leaders; set clear metrics; review quarterly
- Frequency: Regular; can adjust if wrong

**Crisis decisions** (time-sensitive, uncertain):
- Respond to unexpected events: customer loss, executive departure, product failure
- Process: Gather facts quickly; assemble crisis team; communicate frequently
- Frequency: Unpredictable; must decide with incomplete information

**Cultural decisions** (slow impact, foundational):
- Set norms and values: hiring, promotion, rewarding behavior, addressing misconduct
- Process: Be consistent; recognize that actions are watched
- Frequency: Regular; compound over time
