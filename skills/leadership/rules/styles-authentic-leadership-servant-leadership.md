**Authentic Leadership**: Leaders who are aware of their values, lead in alignment with those values, and encourage others to do the same.

Components:
- **Self-awareness**: Understanding your core values, strengths, weaknesses
- **Relational transparency**: Being genuine, transparent, not hiding behind a persona
- **Balanced processing**: Considering diverse perspectives, soliciting input, integrating feedback
- **Internalized moral perspective**: Acting on values regardless of external pressures

Impact: Builds trust through authenticity; creates psychological safety; enables people to bring their whole selves to work.

**Servant Leadership**: Leaders who prioritize serving others' needs, empowerment, and growth over their own advancement.

Key practices:
- **Listening**: Deeply understanding what others need and want
- **Empathy**: Genuinely caring about others' welfare and development
- **Healing**: Helping people recover from harm or conflict
- **Awareness**: Being alert to organizational dynamics and impacts
- **Persuasion**: Using influence rather than authority
- **Conceptualization**: Thinking about bigger vision, not just immediate problems
- **Stewardship**: Holding organization in trust for future
- **Commitment to growth**: Investing in people's development
- **Community building**: Creating sense of belonging

Impact: Builds deep loyalty; develops people; creates healthy culture; enables long-term sustainability.
