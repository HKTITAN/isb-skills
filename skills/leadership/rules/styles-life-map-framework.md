The Life MAP framework helps leaders understand their core beliefs, guiding principles, vision, and strategy for leadership effectiveness.

**Components**:

**1. Core Beliefs**: Fundamental assumptions about human nature, relationships, how the world works
- Example: "People are inherently good and want to contribute" vs. "People are self-interested and need strong oversight"
- Your beliefs drive how you lead; misalignment between espoused values and actual beliefs creates inauthenticity

**2. Guiding Principles**: Values and ethical standards that guide behavior
- Personal principles: integrity, family, learning, growth
- Leadership principles: transparency, empathy, accountability
- When principles conflict, you face dilemmas requiring conscious choice

**3. Vision**: Desired future state—what impact do you want to have? Who do you want to become as a leader?
- Personal: "I want to be known as a developer of leaders"
- Organizational: "We create a culture where people do their best work"
- Vision gives purpose and direction

**4. Strategy**: How you will operationalize your vision; what actions, capabilities, relationships you need to develop

**Using Life MAP**: Spend time clarifying your beliefs, principles, and vision. Then audit alignment: Does how you spend time match your vision? Do your decisions reflect your principles? Are you developing the capabilities your strategy requires?
