**How conflicts escalate**:

1. Initial disagreement: Different perspectives or interests
2. Positions harden; parties become more committed to their view
3. Threats and ultimatums; emotional heat increases
4. Relationship deteriorates; personal attacks begin
5. Third parties become involved; sides form
6. Violence or breakdown (in severe cases)

**De-escalation strategies**:

- Acknowledge legitimate interests on both sides
- Use cooling-off periods; don't try to resolve hot conflicts
- Separate people from problems; criticize ideas, not character
- Build trust through small commitments, follow-through, transparency
- Find common ground and shared interests
- Use neutral mediators for high-stakes conflicts
- Focus on future outcomes, not past blame
