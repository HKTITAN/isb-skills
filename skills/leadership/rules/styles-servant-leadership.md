Servant leadership flips the traditional hierarchy: the leader serves the team's needs rather than the team serving the leader's agenda.

**Key principles**:
- Leader's primary job is removing obstacles, developing people, creating conditions for team success
- Listen deeply to understand what people need
- Prioritize others' growth and wellbeing alongside organizational goals
- Build shared purpose and community
- Exercise power responsibly with humility

**When servant leadership works**: Teams with high autonomy, knowledge work, values-driven organizations. Less effective in crisis or with teams requiring heavy structure.
