Authentic leaders are self-aware, values-driven, transparent, and impact others positively through genuine connection rather than manipulation.

**Key characteristics**:
- **Self-awareness**: Clear about values, strengths, limitations; understand impact on others
- **Values-driven**: Decisions aligned with core values; not just pursuing power or money
- **Transparency**: Honest about challenges, mistakes, limitations; builds trust
- **Accountability**: Own decisions and outcomes; don't blame others
- **Relationship-focused**: Genuine interest in others' development; serve others, not self

**Building authenticity**: Don't create a leadership persona separate from your authentic self. This creates cognitive dissonance and burns people out. Instead: Know yourself deeply, clarify your values, communicate honestly, admit mistakes, invest in genuine relationships.
