High-potential executives sometimes derail—fail to deliver, get fired, or plateau. Common patterns:

**1. Arrogance/Overconfidence**
- Causes: Early success creates illusion of invulnerability; feedback filtered by yes-people
- Outcome: Ignores warning signs; makes reckless decisions
- Prevention: Maintain intellectual humility; seek external perspectives; invite challenge

**2. Poor relationships/Inability to build coalitions**
- Causes: Arrogance, impatience, lack of empathy, poor listening
- Outcome: People actively undermine; isolated from information; can't mobilize support
- Prevention: Invest genuinely in relationships; practice empathy; build networks intentionally

**3. Narrow expertise/Inability to adapt**
- Causes: Built career on one functional expertise; default to what worked before
- Outcome: Struggles in new role; can't flex style; obsolete skills
- Prevention: Seek stretch assignments outside expertise; continuous learning; mentoring in new areas

**4. Failure to develop direct reports**
- Causes: Impatience, fear of being replaced, over-involvement
- Outcome: No bench strength; organization can't grow; people leave; reputation tarnished
- Prevention: Make development non-negotiable; measure success on who you develop; let people fail safely

**5. Poor judgment/Ethical lapses**
- Causes: Pressure, rationalization, loss of perspective, unclear values
- Outcome: Legal, reputational, relationship damage
- Prevention: Clarify values; accountability; integrity non-negotiable

**6. Resistance to change/Inability to lead transformation**
- Causes: Attachment to status quo, fear, overconfidence in existing model
- Outcome: Misses market shifts; organization stagnates
- Prevention: Embrace learning; expose yourself to different perspectives; develop adaptive capacity
