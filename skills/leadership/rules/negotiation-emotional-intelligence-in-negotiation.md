Emotional dynamics significantly impact negotiations:

- **Anger**: Can be strategic (conveys seriousness) but often backfires (creates defensiveness, relationship damage)
- **Fear**: Often drives concessions; manage your own fear and leverage other's anxiety appropriately
- **Trust**: Essential for creative problem-solving; builds through consistent behavior and transparency
- **Liking**: People are more flexible and generous with those they like; build genuine rapport

**Self-regulation**: Manage your emotions rather than letting them manage you. Stay calm even when provoked. Use silence and pauses to think rather than reacting immediately.

**Empathy**: Understand other party's constraints and concerns; this enables creative solutions and reduces adversarial dynamics.
