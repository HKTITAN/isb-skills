---
title: "ZOPA: Zone of Possible Agreement"
impact: CRITICAL
impactDescription: "Core MBA framework, foundational to domain"
tags: agreement, possible, zone, zopa
---

**Definition**: The range between your reservation price and theirs where a deal is possible.

- Your reservation price: Worst deal you'd accept (equivalent to BATNA)
- Their reservation price: Worst deal they'd accept (equivalent to their BATNA)

**Positive ZOPA**: Range where both parties can agree and gain value over BATNA.

**Negative ZOPA**: No overlap; no deal possible that benefits both parties over their BATNAs.

**Example**:
- You'll pay up to $100K for supplier deal (your BATNA is $100K elsewhere)
- They'll accept as low as $80K (their BATNA is $80K)
- ZOPA = $80K-$100K; any price in this range works for both

**Constructing ZOPA strategically**:

1. Maximize the size of ZOPA (expand it through creative problem-solving: different payment terms, phasing, bundles)
2. Claim your share through: Information about your BATNA, anchoring, making good proposals

**Information asymmetry**: Each side often doesn't know the other's BATNA. This creates negotiation space. Revealing your true BATNA may lower your power; but credible signals of good BATNA strengthen it.
