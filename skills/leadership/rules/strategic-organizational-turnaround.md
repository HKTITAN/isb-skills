**Phase 1: Stabilization** (3-6 months)
- Stop the bleeding: conserve cash, shore up core
- Fire clearly misaligned leaders (poison culture faster than individuals)
- Don't make big strategic changes yet; you don't understand the business

**Phase 2: Understanding** (6-12 months)
- Get close to customers and operations
- Identify what works, what's broken
- Find quick wins to build momentum
- Hire experienced operators if you're from outside

**Phase 3: Strategic repositioning** (12-24 months)
- Make bigger changes: customer mix, product mix, go-to-market
- Based on understanding from Phase 2

**Phase 4: Growth** (24+ months)
- Pursue growth only after fundamentals are fixed

Most turnaround leaders fail by rushing Phase 3. Stabilization and understanding can't be rushed.
