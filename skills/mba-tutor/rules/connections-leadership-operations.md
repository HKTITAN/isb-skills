**Change management enables process improvement:**
- Operations: Identified 20% efficiency improvement through new process
- Leadership: Must communicate why, engage resistance, build new capabilities
- Without leadership, operations change creates organizational friction and failure
- Example: Digital transformation fails technically 10% of the time, fails organizationally 90% of the time

**Organizational design supports operational excellence:**
- Command-and-control (military model) works for standardized tasks
- Empowerment (Apple Genius Bar) required for customized service
- Cross-functional teams enable innovation; siloes create local optimization
