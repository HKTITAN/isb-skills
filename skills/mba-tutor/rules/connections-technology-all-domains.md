**Digital transformation affects every function:**
- **Finance**: Automation of accounting, FP&A, financial planning → headcount reduction → cost advantage
- **Marketing**: Data analytics enable precise targeting, attribution, personalization → higher conversion, lower CAC
- **Operations**: AI/automation of routine tasks, IoT monitoring of supply chain → quality improvement, lower costs
- **Strategy**: Digital disruption creates new competitive dynamics (e.g., e-commerce disrupting retail)
- **Leadership**: Remote work changes organizational culture; distributed teams require different coordination

**Technology creates new business models:**
- Platform model (Uber, Airbnb) enabled by smartphone technology and geo-tracking
- Subscription model enabled by SaaS infrastructure and cloud computing
- Marketplace model enabled by digital identity, payment processing, reviews
