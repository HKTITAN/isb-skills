- **Income Statement**: Revenue, costs, profitability; reveals operational efficiency
- **Balance Sheet**: Assets, liabilities, equity; reveals financial position and health
- **Cash Flow Statement**: Where money actually moves; critical reality check on profitability

**Integration**: Profitable companies can go bankrupt (cash flow problem); unprofitable companies can have strong balance sheets (investing for future).
