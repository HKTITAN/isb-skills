Company built for one model (founder-led, handmade, specialized) struggles to scale: culture changes, quality inconsistency, founder burnout, operational systems inadequate, cost structure breaks

**Examples**: Fast-casual restaurant scaling, consulting firms going global

**Strategic lesson**: Scaling requires fundamental changes to how you operate; can't just do more of what worked at small scale.
