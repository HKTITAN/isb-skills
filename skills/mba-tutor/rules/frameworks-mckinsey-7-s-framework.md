Diagnose organizational alignment:
- **Strategy**: Direction and objectives
- **Structure**: Formal organization
- **Systems**: Processes and procedures
- **Skills**: Capabilities and competencies
- **Style**: Culture and decision-making norms
- **Staff**: People and talent
- **Shared values**: Core beliefs

**Insight**: Change in one S requires changes in others; misalignment creates friction and failure.
