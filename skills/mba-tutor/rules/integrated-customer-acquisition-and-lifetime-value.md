- **CAC/LTV ratio** (finance/economics concern) determines sustainability
- **Marketing channels** (email, paid search, social) have different CAC/LTV profiles
- **Product decisions** (features, quality) affect LTV via retention
- **Pricing** (operations/finance) affects both CAC (customer perception of value) and LTV (customer satisfaction)
