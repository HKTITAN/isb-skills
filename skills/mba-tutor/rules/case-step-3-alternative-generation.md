**Develop at least 3 credible options:**
Generate multiple alternatives before evaluating. Common mistake: Anchoring on obvious solution, missing creative alternatives.

**Frameworks for generating alternatives:**
- **Existing playbooks**: What have competitors done? What have other industries done?
- **Constraint relaxation**: What if we had unlimited budget? What if we had no budget? What if we changed timeline?
- **Opposite thinking**: Instead of "how to cut costs," ask "how to increase revenue?" (Different solutions)
- **Customer perspective**: What would customers prefer? (Often reveals options you overlooked)

**Evaluate each alternative on:**
- **Feasibility**: Can we actually execute? Do we have required capabilities, capital, timeline?
- **Impact**: How much does this move the needle on the key problem?
- **Risk**: What could go wrong? How would we recover?
- **Fit with strategy**: Does this align with our positioning and capabilities?

**Avoid traps:**
- **False dichotomy**: Presenting only two options, often good vs bad
- **Status quo bias**: Treating "do nothing" as option when it's actually decline
- **Unrealistic options**: Technically possible but practically impossible given constraints

**Output**: 3+ alternatives with pros/cons summary for each.
