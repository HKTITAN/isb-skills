- **Conceptual**: DCF method, comparable company, revenue multiples
- **Applied**: Value a simple business (coffee shop), then complicate with growth
- **Socratic**: "If we grow 20% instead of 10%, how does valuation change?" → Discover leverage of growth in valuations
- **Common mistake**: One number valuation; help see valuation is range of possibilities based on assumptions
