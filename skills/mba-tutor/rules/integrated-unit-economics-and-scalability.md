title: "Unit Economics and Scalability"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: economics, entrepreneurship, finance, scalability, unit
---

- **Unit economics** (revenue per unit - cost per unit) determine if scaling helps or hurts
- **Negative unit economics**: Scale makes losses bigger
- **Positive unit economics with high CAC/LTV**: Scaling requires massive capital
- **Healthy unit economics (3x+ LTV/CAC)**: Scaling becomes capital-efficient
