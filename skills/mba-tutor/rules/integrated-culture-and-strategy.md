---
title: "Culture and Strategy"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: culture, leadership, strategy, technology
---

- **Culture is how things get done when nobody's watching**: Reflects values, decision-making norms, risk tolerance
- **Strong cultures enable strategy**: Decentralized decision-making requires shared values and clear principles
- **Culture-strategy misalignment**: Claiming innovation but punishing failure; claiming customer focus but rewarding internal politics
- **Leadership's role**: Model desired culture, hire for fit, build traditions/rituals, address misalignment quickly
