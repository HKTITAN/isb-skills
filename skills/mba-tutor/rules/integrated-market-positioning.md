**STP Framework:**
- **Segmentation**: Which customer groups are distinct? Different needs, willingness to pay, behaviors?
- **Targeting**: Which segments to serve? Profitable? Large enough? Defensible?
- **Positioning**: What value do we deliver? How do we differentiate vs competitors?

**Positioning requires integration**: Product must deliver on promise (operations), pricing must reflect value (finance and economics), messaging must resonate (marketing), organizational structure must support (leadership).
