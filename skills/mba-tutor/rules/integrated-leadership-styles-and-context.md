---
title: "Leadership Styles and Context"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: context, leadership, styles
---

- **Directive**: Clear goals, close supervision; effective in crises or low-skill teams; demotivating for skilled teams
- **Participative**: Engage team in decision-making; effective for complex problems and engaged teams; slow decision-making
- **Transformational**: Inspire and energize; effective for major change and innovation; risk of unsustainable expectations
- **Context matters**: No one style optimal; match to situation
