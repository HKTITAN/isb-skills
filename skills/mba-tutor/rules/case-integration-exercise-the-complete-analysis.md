When analyzing a company or business problem, follow this complete framework:

1. **Situation**: Use 5Cs, SWOT, Five Forces; understand context
2. **Strategy**: What is company trying to do? How differentiated vs competitors?
3. **Operations**: How does company execute? Is operational model aligned with strategy?
4. **Finance**: Is financial model sustainable? What are unit economics?
5. **Marketing**: How does company attract and retain customers? Is positioning working?
6. **Economics**: What market dynamics exist? Are they favorable?
7. **Leadership**: Is organization designed and led for success? Is culture aligned?
8. **Technology**: What role does technology play? Is it enabler or differentiator?
9. **Cross-domain connections**: How do choices in one domain affect others?
10. **Recommendation**: Based on integrated analysis, what should company do?

This complete view separates MBA thinking from fragmented domain expertise.
