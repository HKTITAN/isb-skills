- **Just-in-time**: Low inventory costs but vulnerable to disruption (Finance likes; Risk concerns)
- **Vertical integration**: Control quality, reduce costs but loses flexibility (Strategy vs Finance tradeoff)
- **Outsourcing**: Flexibility and capital efficiency but loss of control (Finance likes; Quality risk)
