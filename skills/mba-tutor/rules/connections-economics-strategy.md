**Market structures drive competitive dynamics:**
- **Perfect competition** (commodities): Price-takers; no differentiation possible; compete on cost/efficiency
- **Monopolistic competition** (restaurants): Some differentiation possible; price-makers but elastic demand; brands/location matter
- **Oligopoly** (airlines, cars): Few players; interdependent decisions; capacity management critical
- **Monopoly** (utilities, patents): Price-maker; inelastic demand; regulatory concern
- **Strategy varies by structure**: Differentiation impossible in commodity; essential in monopolistic competition; capacity/network effects critical in oligopoly

**Network effects create economic moats:**
- Two-sided platforms (Visa, Mastercard): Value increases as both sides grow
- Standards network effects (Windows, Microsoft Office): Value from compatibility and content
- Economic implication: Winner takes most; high switching costs; durable competitive advantage
