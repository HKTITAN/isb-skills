impact: MEDIUM

---

- **Cost of quality**: Investing in quality costs money (better materials, more testing, better processes)
- **Cost of poor quality**: Defects, returns, lost reputation, customer acquisition cost increases
- **Economic optimum**: Not zero defects (too expensive); point where cost of quality + cost of poor quality is minimized
- **Integration**: Marketing claims quality; Operations must deliver; Finance must accept cost tradeoff
