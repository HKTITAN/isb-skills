- **Automation of routine tasks**: Reduces cost and errors; requires upfront investment; displaces workers (leadership challenge)
- **Data analytics**: Enables personalization, targeting, early warning systems (competitive advantage)
- **New business models**: Digital platforms, subscription models, ecosystem plays
- **Integration**: Technology strategy should align with business strategy; don't digitize bad processes
