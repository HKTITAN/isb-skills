**The 5Cs Framework:**
1. **Company**: What are the business's core competencies, constraints, financial position, strategic positioning?
2. **Customers**: Who are they? What do they value? What are their decision criteria?
3. **Competitors**: Who competes? On what dimensions? What are their strengths/weaknesses?
4. **Collaborators**: Suppliers, partners, distribution channels; are relationships strong or vulnerable?
5. **Context**: Market trends, macro environment, regulatory landscape, technological change

**SWOT Analysis:**
- **Strengths**: Internal capabilities, resources, brands, relationships
- **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations, cost disadvantages, outdated technology
- **Opportunities**: Market trends, unmet customer needs, adjacencies, regulatory changes
- **Threats**: Competitive moves, market shifts, disruptive technology, economic downturns

**Industry Analysis (Porter's Five Forces):**
1. **Supplier power**: Can suppliers charge high prices? Are there substitutes? How concentrated is supply?
2. **Buyer power**: Can customers demand low prices? Are there alternatives? How concentrated is demand?
3. **Threat of new entrants**: What barriers exist? (Capital requirements, economies of scale, brand, switching costs, regulation)
4. **Threat of substitutes**: Alternative ways to solve customer problem? Different industry?
5. **Competitive rivalry**: Number of competitors? Differentiation? Growth rate? Diversity of competitors?

**Output**: List key strategic facts; identify constraints and opportunities; understand competitive environment.
