**Recommendation is criteria-based evaluation:**

**Define evaluation criteria (weight if quantifying):**
- Financial: Profitability, cash flow, ROI, payback period
- Strategic fit: Competitive advantage, defensibility, market positioning
- Operational feasibility: Timeline, resource requirement, execution risk
- Customer impact: Value delivered, adoption likelihood, retention
- Risk tolerance: Downside risk acceptable to organization?

**Example evaluation:**
| Criterion | Weight | Option A | Option B | Option C |
|-----------|--------|----------|----------|----------|
| Financial ROI | 30% | High (25%+) | Moderate (15%) | High (20%) |
| Strategic fit | 25% | Low (fits legacy) | High (new capability) | Moderate |
| Execution risk | 25% | Low (proven) | High (unproven) | Moderate |
| Customer impact | 20% | Modest | High | High |
| **Weighted score** | 100% | 14.5 | 17.5 | 16.0 |

**Recommendation structure:**
1. Recommended option and why
2. Key assumptions (what must be true for this to work?)
3. How it addresses root cause vs just treating symptoms
4. Why superior to alternatives (not just positive; comparative)
5. Contingency plans (what if key assumptions don't hold?)

**Avoid:**
- Recommendation without supporting analysis
- Hedging ("Either A or B could work")
- Ignoring key tradeoffs (every recommendation has downsides)

**Output**: Clear recommendation with supporting rationale.
