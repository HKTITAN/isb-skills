**Durable competitive advantages come from:**
1. **Cost advantage**: Scale economies, proprietary processes, location, procurement power
2. **Differentiation**: Brand, quality, service, customization, network effects
3. **Switching costs**: Ecosystem lock-in, learning investments, integration with customer operations

**Defensibility**: How long before competitors replicate advantage?
- **Easily replicated** (features): 6-12 months
- **Moderately defensible** (brand, scale): 2-5 years
- **Highly defensible** (network effects, ecosystem, patents): 5+ years

**Integration**: Finance wants to invest in defensible advantages (higher valuation multiples); Marketing wants to communicate advantages; Operations must deliver on promised differentiation.
