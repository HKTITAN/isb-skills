Company entering new market: Can enter low-cost (undercut incumbent) or differentiated (avoid head-to-head). Low-cost requires cost structure incumbent can't match; differentiated requires accepting smaller market. Most entries fail by trying both simultaneously.

**Examples**: Southwest Airlines (low-cost in US), Lexus (premium in luxury), Huawei (different positioning in different markets)

**Strategic lesson**: Choose one dimension of competition; be disciplined about target customer.
