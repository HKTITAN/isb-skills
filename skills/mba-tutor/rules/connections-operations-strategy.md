**Supply chain design reflects strategic positioning:**
- Cost leadership (Walmart): Highly efficient supply chain, centralized distribution
- Differentiation (luxury brand): Supply chain preserves exclusivity, quality
- Speed/responsiveness (Zara): Agile supply chain allows fast trend-to-shelf
- Strategic choice drives operational design, capital allocation, KPIs

**Operational constraints limit strategic options:**
- Can't pursue low-cost if operations require hand-crafted quality
- Can't pursue differentiation if scaled manufacturing eliminates customization
- Strategic flexibility requires operational flexibility (costs money)
