Do company resources create competitive advantage?
1. **Value**: Does resource help company serve customers or manage costs?
2. **Rarity**: Do competitors have similar resources?
3. **Inimitability**: Can competitors replicate this resource?
4. **Organization**: Is company organized to leverage resource?

**Output**: Sustainable competitive advantage requires all four.
