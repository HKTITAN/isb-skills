Classify products/business units by growth and market share:
- **Stars** (high growth, high share): Invest; likely future profits
- **Cash cows** (low growth, high share): Milk; generate cash; may need protection
- **Question marks** (high growth, low share): Decide whether to invest or divest
- **Dogs** (low growth, low share): Divest unless strategic reason to keep

**Application**: Portfolio allocation decisions; resource prioritization.
