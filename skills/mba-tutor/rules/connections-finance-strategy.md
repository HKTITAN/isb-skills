title: "Finance ↔ Strategy"
impact: CRITICAL

**Valuation connects to competitive advantage:**
- Discounted cash flow (DCF) values a company based on future cash flows
- Sustainable competitive advantage → durable high margins → higher value
- Strategic positioning affects growth rate and risk profile
- Example: Apple's ecosystem creates switching costs (competitive moat) → higher margins (15%+ vs 5% commoditized phones) → lower risk of disruption → higher valuation multiples

**Growth and financial constraints interact:**
- Strategy says "expand to 3 new markets"; Finance says "that requires $50M capital"
- Different financing (debt vs equity) affects strategic options
- Profitability and reinvestment rates determine organic growth
