impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"

---

**Pricing strategy uses elasticity and behavioral insights:**
- Economics: Price elasticity determines optimal markup (perfect competition: MC=P; monopoly: MR=MC)
- Marketing adds: Brand preference, reference pricing, anchoring, loss aversion
- Example: Netflix pricing increases show that consumers exhibit low elasticity (willing to pay more for convenience); premium tier offers anchoring effect for standard tier

**Market segmentation is economically informed:**
- Identify customers with different elasticity levels (willingness to pay)
- Price discriminate to capture consumer surplus
- Marketing tactics (positioning, messaging) support economic price differentiation
