- **Predict outcomes**: "If price increases 10%, what happens to sales?" Shows causal thinking
- **Transfer to new case**: Can explain similar situation in different context
- **Identify tradeoffs**: "This creates advantage in X but disadvantage in Y"
- **Disagree respectfully**: Can challenge frameworks while showing mastery
