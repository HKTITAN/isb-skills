- **Conceptual**: CAC, LTV, payback period, LTV/CAC ratio
- **Applied**: Calculate for local business (Starbucks CAC vs LTV); compare to company under study
- **Integration**: How does pricing affect LTV? How does marketing channel affect CAC? How do unit economics influence valuation?
- **Common mistake**: Treating unit economics as fixed; help see how decisions change them
