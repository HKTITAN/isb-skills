**Translate decision into action:**

**Timeline and sequencing:**
- What's the critical path? (What must happen first?)
- Quick wins vs foundational work (Build momentum early)
- When do we expect to see results? (3 months? 1 year?)

**Resources required:**
- Budget: How much capital? Capex vs opex?
- People: What skills? Internal vs external? Training needed?
- Organizational changes: New roles? Reporting lines? Incentives?

**Metrics and monitoring:**
- How do we track progress? (Leading indicators, not just lagging)
- Decision gates: When would we pivot or accelerate?
- What would constitute success? Quantify.
- What would prompt course correction? Define thresholds.

**Risk mitigation:**
- What are the largest risks? (Customer adoption, execution, market change)
- How would we mitigate each?
- Contingency plans: If this doesn't work, what's plan B?

**Stakeholder management:**
- Who needs to support this? (Finance, operations, customers, board)
- How do we communicate? (Persuade, not just inform)
- What incentives align people with success?

**Output**: Actionable plan with clear ownership, timeline, metrics, and contingencies.
