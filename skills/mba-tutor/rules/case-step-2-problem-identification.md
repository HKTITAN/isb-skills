**Distinguish symptoms from root causes:**
- **Symptom**: "Revenue declining" or "customer churn increasing"
- **Root cause investigation**: Why are customers leaving? Is it price, product quality, service, competition, market contraction?
- Question assumptions: Are we sure about the diagnosis? What would disprove this hypothesis?

**Key questions for problem identification:**
- What is the key decision or challenge? (Avoid trying to solve everything)
- Is this a problem the business owner/CEO recognizes? (Sometimes not)
- What constraints exist? (Budget, time, capability, market dynamics)
- Is the problem solvable? (Some problems require accepting tradeoffs, not solving)

**Types of problems:**
- **Performance problems**: Why isn't the business achieving targets?
- **Strategic choice problems**: Which direction should the company move? (Market entry, product launch, M&A)
- **Operational problems**: How to improve efficiency, quality, speed?
- **Organizational problems**: How to structure, incentivize, and coordinate teams?

**Output**: Clear problem statement; consensus on whether this is the right problem to solve.
