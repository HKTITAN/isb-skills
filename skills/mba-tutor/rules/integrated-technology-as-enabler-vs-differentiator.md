---
title: "Technology as Enabler vs Differentiator"
impact: CRITICAL
impactDescription: "Core MBA framework, foundational to domain"
tags: differentiator, enabler, frameworks, strategy, technology
---

- **Enabler**: Technology allows business model (e.g., cloud computing enables SaaS)
- **Differentiator**: Technology creates competitive advantage (e.g., Amazon's recommendation engine, Tesla's battery tech)
- **Strategic question**: Is this technology something competitors can replicate? If yes, it's cost of doing business; if no, it's differentiation
