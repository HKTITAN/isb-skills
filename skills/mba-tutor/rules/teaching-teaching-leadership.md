- **Conceptual**: Leadership styles, change management, culture
- **Applied**: Observe leadership in real companies (public statements, decisions, outcomes)
- **Self-reflection**: How would you lead through change? What's your natural style? What situations require different style?
- **Common mistake**: Thinking leadership is about charisma; help distinguish from decision-making, accountability, culture-building
