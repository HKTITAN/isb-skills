- **Dominant strategy**: Optimal move regardless of competitor response
- **Prisoner's dilemma**: Individually rational moves lead to collectively worse outcomes (e.g., price wars)
- **Tit-for-tat**: Respond in kind to competitor moves (cooperative but punishes defection)
- **First-mover advantage** vs **follower advantage**: Depends on industry dynamics, switching costs, capital requirements
