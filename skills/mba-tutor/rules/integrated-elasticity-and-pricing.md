- **Price elasticity of demand**: % change in quantity demanded ÷ % change in price
- **Elastic demand** (e > 1): Consumers price-sensitive; raise prices → lower revenue
- **Inelastic demand** (e < 1): Consumers not price-sensitive; raise prices → higher revenue
- **Marketing's role**: Create emotional attachment, reduce elasticity; allows pricing power
- **Examples**: Gas (inelastic, addiction-like) vs restaurant meals (elastic, many alternatives)
