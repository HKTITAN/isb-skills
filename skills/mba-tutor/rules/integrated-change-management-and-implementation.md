- **Technical implementation** (new process, new system) is often 10% of effort; **organizational change** (adoption, capability building, mindset shift) is 90%
- **Common failure modes**: Announcing change without engaging hearts and minds; not addressing resistance; insufficient capability building; changing too much at once
- **Success factors**: Clear compelling vision, visible leadership commitment, early wins to build momentum, multiple communication channels, support structure
