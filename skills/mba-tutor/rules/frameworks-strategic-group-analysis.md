Map competitors on two relevant dimensions:
- **Luxury car market**: Map on price vs innovation
- **Airlines**: Map on cost vs service
- **Restaurants**: Map on price vs quality

**Insight**: Companies in same strategic group compete directly; companies in different groups have different competitive dynamics. Switching between groups requires fundamental change.
