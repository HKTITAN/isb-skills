Assess attractiveness of industry:
1. Supplier power: Can suppliers raise prices? Are there alternatives?
2. Buyer power: Can customers demand lower prices? Are there alternatives?
3. Threat of entrants: What barriers prevent new competitors?
4. Threat of substitutes: Can customers solve problem differently?
5. Competitive rivalry: How many competitors? Is industry fragmented or concentrated?

**Application**: Assess each force; identify which are attractive and which are threats.
