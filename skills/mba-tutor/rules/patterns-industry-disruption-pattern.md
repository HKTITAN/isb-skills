Many cases follow disruption template: Incumbent optimizes for today; entrant targets underserved customer; incumbent dismisses entrant; entrant improves product; incumbent's cost structure prevents competitive response; entrant wins.

**Examples**: Netflix (video rental), Uber (taxis), Airbnb (hotels), Tesla (cars)

**Strategic lesson**: What looks uncompetitive today might be tomorrow's threat.
