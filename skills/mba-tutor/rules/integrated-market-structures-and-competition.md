- **Perfect competition**: No differentiation possible; compete on price; zero long-term profits (unlikely real-world)
- **Monopolistic competition**: Differentiation possible; firms are price-makers (restaurants, local services)
- **Oligopoly**: Few competitors; interdependent; capacity/coordination critical (airlines, auto)
- **Monopoly**: One player; high barriers; price-maker; regulatory risk
- **Strategy varies by structure**: Know your market structure to set realistic competitive strategy
