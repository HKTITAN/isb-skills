- **Functional organization**: Grouped by expertise (Finance, Marketing, Operations); efficient for stable products; poor cross-functional coordination
- **Product organization**: Teams around products; better coordination; risk of duplicated functions
- **Matrix organization**: Dual reporting lines (function + product); attempt to balance efficiency + coordination; confusing accountability
- **Integration**: Organization structure affects decision speed, cost structure, culture, who gets promoted
