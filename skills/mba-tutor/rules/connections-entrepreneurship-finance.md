---
title: "Entrepreneurship ↔ Finance"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: entrepreneurship, finance, technology
---

**Funding decisions enable (or constrain) growth strategy:**
- Bootstrapped startup: Sustainable unit economics required; scaling slower but more capital-efficient
- Venture-backed startup: Can pursue growth at expense of profitability (blitzscaling); unit economics secondary if market is winner-take-most
- Public company: Capital markets provide liquidity and access to capital; public market pressures force short-term earnings focus

**Unit economics determine financing path:**
- SaaS with 30% CAC/LTV ratio: Likely self-sustaining, venture capital unnecessary
- Uber/Lyft with <1x CAC/LTV: Burning capital; requires ongoing VC funding to achieve scale economies
