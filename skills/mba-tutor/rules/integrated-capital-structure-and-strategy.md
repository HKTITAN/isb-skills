- **Debt financing**: Cheaper than equity (tax deductible) but creates fixed obligations; limits strategic flexibility
- **Equity financing**: Expensive but provides flexibility; dilutes founders
- **Optimal capital structure**: Balances cost of capital with financial risk and strategic flexibility
