Identify growth options:
1. **Market penetration**: Sell more of current product to current market (low risk, limited upside)
2. **Product development**: Develop new products for current market (medium risk/reward)
3. **Market development**: Sell current product to new market (medium risk/reward)
4. **Diversification**: New product to new market (high risk, high reward)

**Application**: Balance portfolio across risk/reward; assess strategic fit.
