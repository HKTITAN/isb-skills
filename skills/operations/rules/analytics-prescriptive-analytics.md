**Optimization Fundamentals**: Choose best action(s) given constraints and objectives.
- Components: Decision variables, objective function, constraints, parameters
- Deterministic: Known parameters; solved to global optimum
- Stochastic: Probability distributions for uncertain parameters; considers risk
- Robust: Finds solution performing well under multiple scenarios without optimizing for specific one

**Digital Twins**: Virtual replica of physical system used for optimization and what-if analysis before real-world implementation.

**Constraint Relaxation**: When infeasible, relax constraints systematically to understand trade-offs. Which constraint removal gives largest improvement?

**Applications in Fashion Retail**: Optimize assortment and allocation across stores and seasons given uncertainty in demand and price elasticity

**Applications in Energy**: Optimize renewable energy scheduling and storage given variable wind/solar generation and demand patterns

**Sensitivity Analysis**: Tests solution robustness to parameter changes; identifies critical parameters driving solution
