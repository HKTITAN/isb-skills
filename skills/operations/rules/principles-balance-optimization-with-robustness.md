Optimizing for normal conditions can create fragility:
- Cost-minimized supply chains are vulnerable to disruptions
- Tightly scheduled operations have no buffer for variability
- Build in resilience: redundancy, flexibility, visibility, contingency
- Solve for cost subject to resilience/robustness constraints
