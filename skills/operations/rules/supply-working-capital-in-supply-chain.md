**Receivables Optimization**: Balance credit terms to attract customers against minimizing bad debt and cash flow impact. Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) metric.

**Payables Management**: Extend payment terms to suppliers (Days Payable Outstanding, DPO) to improve cash flow while maintaining relationships.

**Inventory Reduction**: Better forecasting, faster turnover, reduced safety stock, supplier partnerships frees capital.

**Cash Conversion Cycle**: DPO - DSO - Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO). Shorter cycles free capital.
