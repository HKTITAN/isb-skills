**Project Charter**: Authorization document establishing purpose, objectives, high-level requirements.
- Business case: Why matters, expected benefits
- Success criteria: Measurable targets for scope, time, cost, quality
- High-level risks and assumptions

**Scope Statement**: Clear definition of included and excluded work.
- Deliverables: Tangible outputs
- Acceptance criteria: How to determine if meets requirements
- Exclusions: Explicitly state what's not in scope

**Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)**: Hierarchical decomposition into work packages.
- Top level: Overall project
- Subsequent levels: Major phases, components, deliverables, activities
- Ensures nothing is missed; enables clear responsibility assignment
