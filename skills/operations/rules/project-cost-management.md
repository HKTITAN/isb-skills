**Estimation Techniques**:

- Analogous: Use historical data from similar projects
- Parametric: Cost per unit of work (e.g., $/mile)
- Bottom-up: Estimate each activity and roll up
- Three-point: Optimistic, most likely, pessimistic

**Budget Baseline**: Approved cost plan; cumulative over time (S-curve). Changes require change control.

**Earned Value Management (EVM)**: Integrates schedule, cost, and scope.

- PV (Planned Value): Budget for work scheduled to be completed by date
- EV (Earned Value): Budget for work actually completed
- AC (Actual Cost): Real money spent
- SPI (Schedule Performance Index): EV / PV; > 1 means ahead of schedule
- CPI (Cost Performance Index): EV / AC; > 1 means under budget
- EAC (Estimate at Completion): BAC / CPI if productivity continues
- ETC (Estimate to Complete): EAC - AC; remaining budget needed
