No single dimension is always best; operations involves balancing competing objectives:
- Cost vs. Quality: Investing in quality typically reduces total cost through fewer defects and rework
- Cost vs. Speed: Faster response often requires excess capacity or inventory
- Flexibility vs. Efficiency: Standardization improves efficiency; customization requires flexibility
- Responsiveness vs. Efficiency: Responsive chains (distributed inventory) cost more than efficient (centralized)
