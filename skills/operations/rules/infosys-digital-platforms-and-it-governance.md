**Digital Platforms**: Ecosystems connecting suppliers, operations, customers.
- Real-time data exchange enables responsiveness
- Platform governance: who owns data, how is it shared?

**IT Governance**: Policies ensuring IT investments align with business strategy.
- Portfolio management: Balance IT spending across maintenance, incremental improvements, transformational initiatives
- Risk management: Cybersecurity, business continuity
