**Process Capability**: Compare process variation to specification width.
- Cp (potential): (USL - LSL) / (6σ) — assumes process centered
- Cpk (actual): min[(USL - mean)/3σ, (mean - LSL)/3σ] — accounts for centering
- Cpk > 1.33 generally considered capable
- Cpk > 1.67 called "world class"

**DPMO (Defects Per Million Opportunities)**:
- 1 sigma: 308,538 DPMO (69% conforming)
- 2 sigma: 69,146 DPMO (95% conforming)
- 3 sigma: 6,210 DPMO (99.73% conforming)
- 6 sigma: 3.4 DPMO (99.9997% conforming, accounting for 1.5σ process drift)
