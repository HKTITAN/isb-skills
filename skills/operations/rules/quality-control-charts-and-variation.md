## title: "Control Charts and Variation"
impact: MEDIUM
impactDescription: "Supporting concept that enhances domain understanding"
tags: charts, control, variation

**Types of Variation**:

- Common cause: Normal variation inherent in process; random, stable
- Special cause: Unusual variation from assignable cause; requires investigation

**Control Chart Rules**:

- Point beyond 3σ control limits: Investigate (high probability of special cause)
- Run of 8+ points on one side of center line: Trend indicates shift
- Trend of 6 points increasing/decreasing: Process changing systematically

**Control Limits vs. Specification Limits**:

- Control limits (±3σ): Process variation boundaries; for process control
- Specification limits: Customer requirements; for product acceptance
- Process can be in control but not meeting specs (or meeting specs but out of control in future)
