**Demand and Sales Forecasting**:

- Time series methods (ARIMA, Holt-Winters) for stable patterns
- Regression for causal relationships (price, promotions, competitors)
- Machine learning (gradient boosting) for complex relationships
- Ensemble methods: Combine multiple models for robustness

**Inventory Optimization**:

- ABC Analysis: Classify items by value; allocate management intensity
- Demand sensing: Real-time signals (POS, web traffic) for immediate decisions
- Newsvendor model: Trade-off stockout cost vs. overstock cost
- Network optimization: How much to stock at each location given centralization/decentralization trade-off

**Capacity and Workforce Planning**:

- Queuing theory: What staffing level achieves target service level?
- Simulation: What happens under demand variability?
- Regression: Historical relationships between drivers (day of week, weather, events) and demand

**Process Improvement Root Cause Analysis**:

- 5 Whys: Systematic drilling from symptom to root
- Fishbone diagram: Structured categorization of possible causes
- Pareto analysis: 80% of problems come from 20% of causes; focus there
- Design of Experiments (DOE): Systematic testing of factor combinations

**Network and Supply Chain Design**:

- Linear/integer programming: Facility location, allocation, routing
- Transportation problem: Optimal shipping from sources to destinations
- Shortest path (Dijkstra): Optimal routing
- Simulation: Test network robustness to disruptions

**Financial and Working Capital Optimization**:

- Scenario analysis: What if demand changes? Cost assumptions?
- Sensitivity analysis: Which variables most affect profitability?
- Cash flow modeling: DIO, DSO, DPO interactions
- Solution: Adapt processes to system rather than heavily customizing system
