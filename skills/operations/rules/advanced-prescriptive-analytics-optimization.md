**Goal**: Recommend best actions given constraints and objectives.

**Problem Formulation**:
- Decision variables: What we're deciding
- Objective function: What we're optimizing (maximize profit, minimize cost)
- Constraints: Limitations on decisions
- Parameter values: Known constants

**Optimization Types**:
- Linear: All functions linear (solved efficiently)
- Integer/Binary: Variables must be integers or 0/1 (harder computationally)
- Non-linear: Non-linear functions (often harder, may have local optima)
- Stochastic: Accounts for uncertainty

**Applications in Operations**:
- Blending problems: Mix ingredients to meet specifications at minimum cost
- Production scheduling: Allocate production across time and products
- Inventory optimization: Determine order quantities and timing
- Facility location: Select warehouse/plant locations and capacities
- Network design: Determine supply chain structure
- Workforce scheduling: Assign workers to shifts
