Local optimization (e.g., procurement cost reduction) often creates problems elsewhere (quality issues, disruption). Consider system-wide impact.
- What upstream effects might our decisions create?
- Are there customer/downstream costs offsetting our savings?
- Could we better optimize by coordinating with trading partners?
