Operations doesn't exist for its own sake; every decision should trace to business impact.
- Cost reduction: Direct impact on profitability, competitiveness
- Quality improvement: Affects customer satisfaction, retention, brand value
- Speed/Responsiveness: Enables market responsiveness, customer service
- Flexibility: Enables adaptation to changing conditions
