Technical solutions fail without people adoption:
- Communicate: Why change is needed and how it benefits workers
- Involve: Employees in designing and piloting improvements
- Train: Provide skills needed for new processes/systems
- Sustain: Celebrate wins, hold accountable, refresh standards
- Culture: Embed improvement mindset; make continuous improvement everyone's job
