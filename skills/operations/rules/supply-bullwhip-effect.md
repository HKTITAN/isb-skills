---
title: "Bullwhip Effect"
impact: CRITICAL
impactDescription: "Core MBA framework, foundational to domain"
tags: bullwhip, effect, operations, technology
---

Small demand fluctuations at retail create increasingly larger swings upstream in supply chain.

**Root Causes**:
- Forecast Updating: Each player forecasts based on orders received, introducing distortion
- Order Batching: Placing larger orders less frequently to reduce transaction costs
- Price Fluctuations: Promotions incentivize buying ahead when prices dip
- Rationing/Shortage Gaming: Customers over-order to ensure availability during scarcity
- Information Asymmetry: Manufacturers don't know retail sales, only wholesale orders

**Mitigation Strategies**:
- Information Sharing: Share point-of-sale data with suppliers
- Vendor-Managed Inventory (VMI): Supplier manages customer inventory based on actual consumption
- CPFR (Collaborative Planning, Forecasting, Replenishment): Joint planning across supply chain
- Stable Pricing: Consistent pricing removes incentive to buy ahead
- Kanban Systems: Visual pull signals reduce large forecasts
- Single Point of Replenishment: Consolidate orders through one distribution center
