**ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)**: Integrated system connecting finance, operations, supply chain, HR.
- Single database reduces silos and redundancy
- Standardized processes enforce consistent workflows
- Real-time visibility supports decision-making
- Challenges: cost, complexity, change management

**CRM (Customer Relationship Management)**: Manages customer interactions, sales pipelines, marketing campaigns.
- Improves customer lifetime value through better service
- Integrates with ERP for order fulfillment

**SCM (Supply Chain Management) Systems**: Specialized for procurement, inventory, demand planning, supplier management.
- Often integrated with ERP or standalone
- Provides visibility across supply chain partners

**Integration Challenges**: Different systems have different data formats and business logic; ETL (Extract-Transform-Load) bridges gaps.
