**Progression from Reactive to Proactive**:
1. **Descriptive**: What happened? Historical data, dashboards, reporting, KPIs. Technologies: databases, BI tools (Tableau, Power BI), reports
2. **Diagnostic**: Why did it happen? Root cause analysis, data exploration, hypothesis testing. Answers "why" by correlating variables
3. **Predictive**: What will happen? Forecasting, pattern recognition, ML models. Builds models from historical data to predict future
4. **Prescriptive**: What should we do? Optimization, simulations, recommendations. Recommends specific actions to optimize outcomes

**Analytics Maturity Benefits**:
- Descriptive: Baseline awareness; exposes problems
- Diagnostic: Understand causes; enables root cause fixes
- Predictive: Anticipate issues; enables proactive response
- Prescriptive: Optimal actions; quantifies impact of decisions

**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**:
- Visualize distributions (histograms, box plots) to understand data; identify outliers, skewness
- Check for missing data; understand patterns (random, systematic)
- Correlation analysis: Which variables related? Multicollinearity issues?
- Segment analysis: Do patterns differ by customer, product, time period? (cohort analysis)
- Temporal patterns: Trends, seasonality, structural breaks

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) Development**:
- Strategic alignment: Does KPI connect to business objective? Can executives act on it?
- Actionable: Can operational team influence the metric? Or is it just a result?
- Measurable: Can we track it? Do we have data? Is it repeatable?
- Leading vs. Lagging: Leading (e.g., demand forecast error) predicts future outcomes; lagging (e.g., profit) is result
- Balance: Mix of lagging (results) and leading (early warning) metrics

**Visualization Principles**:
- Audience: Executives want summaries and trends; operators want details and variability
- Show variance: Error bars, confidence intervals (not just point estimates)
- Context: Benchmark against target, prior period, competitor
- Simplicity: Eliminate non-data ink; don't distort with 3D effects
- Color: Use sparingly; check for colorblind friendly palettes

**Organizational Readiness**: Prescriptive analytics requires:
- Data infrastructure (descriptive capability): Clean, integrated, accessible data
- Skilled talent: Data engineers, analysts, data scientists
- Culture: Accepting data-driven decisions; psychological safety to challenge assumptions
- Governance: Data quality standards, security, ethical use
