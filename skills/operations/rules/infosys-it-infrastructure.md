**On-Premise vs. Cloud**:
- On-premise: Company owns hardware/software; control but high capital and maintenance
- Cloud: Vendor provides; lower upfront cost, scalability, but less control and vendor dependency

**SaaS**: Software via web browser; vendor manages everything.
**PaaS**: Vendor provides development environment; company builds applications.
**IaaS**: Vendor provides computing resources; company manages applications and data.
