Establish KPIs reflecting strategy.
- Process metrics: Efficiency, quality, safety, environmental
- Customer metrics: Delivery reliability, quality, responsiveness, price
- Financial metrics: Profitability, cash flow, ROA
- Regular monitoring; act when metrics drift from targets
