Operations is often viewed as a cost center. Strategic operations creates advantage through:

- Cost leadership: Lowest-cost competitor underprices while maintaining profitability
- Differentiation: Superior quality, reliability, customization, innovation
- Speed and flexibility: Rapid response, quick launches
- Customer responsiveness: Availability, reliability

**Order Winners vs. Order Qualifiers**:

- Order qualifiers: Minimum acceptable performance (must meet or lose deal)
- Order winners: Dimensions that actually win business (beats competitors)
- Example - Aircraft engines: Qualifiers (reliability, safety, certifications); Winners (fuel efficiency, maintenance costs, customization)
