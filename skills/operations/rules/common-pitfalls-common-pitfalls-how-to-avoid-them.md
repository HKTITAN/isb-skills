**Pitfall**: Local optimization destroying global performance
- Solution: Always consider end-to-end system impact

**Pitfall**: Optimizing for normal operations creating fragility
- Solution: Build in resilience, redundancy, buffers

**Pitfall**: Metrics driving wrong behavior
- Solution: Align metrics with strategic objectives; measure outcomes not just activities

**Pitfall**: Poor forecast accuracy cascading through supply chain
- Solution: Invest in forecasting, share demand information, reduce forecast update frequency

**Pitfall**: Ignoring human factors in process improvement
- Solution: Involve employees, communicate why, train thoroughly, celebrate wins

**Pitfall**: Projects systematically over-budget and late
- Solution: Use historical data for estimates, build realistic reserves, manage scope rigorously

**Pitfall**: Capacity mismatches (too much idle time or long wait times)
- Solution: Use queuing theory and simulation to right-size capacity

**Pitfall**: Supply chain bullwhip effect amplifying small demand swings
- Solution: Share information, stabilize pricing, implement VMI/CPFR

**Pitfall**: Quality/cost trade-off misunderstood
- Solution: Recognize investing in quality reduces total cost through fewer defects

**Pitfall**: Over-customizing IT systems preventing upgrades and increasing costs
- Solution: Use standard configurations; customize only for true competitive advantage
