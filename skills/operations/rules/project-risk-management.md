**Risk Identification**: Brainstorm what could go wrong. Categories: technical, schedule, cost, resource, external.

**Qualitative Assessment**:
- Probability (low/medium/high)
- Impact (low/medium/high)
- Risk scoring matrix: probability × impact

**Quantitative Assessment**:
- Expected monetary value: Probability × Impact ($); helps prioritize high-impact risks
- Decision tree analysis: Model decision options and risk branches
- Monte Carlo simulation of duration/cost: Build probabilistic cost/time models incorporating risk
- Risk-adjusted estimates: Add contingency (reserve) to reflect overall project risk

**Contingency and Reserve**:
- Contingency Reserves: Budget and schedule set-asides for known unknowns (identified risks)
- Management Reserve: Set-aside for unknown unknowns (unidentified risks); typically 5-10% of total project cost
- Reserve burn-down: Monitor how much reserve is consumed; high consumption indicates underestimation or new risks

**Response Strategies**:
- Avoid: Eliminate the risk
- Mitigate: Reduce probability or impact
- Transfer: Shift to third party (insurance, outsourcing)
- Accept: Acknowledge with contingency plan

**Risk Reserves**: 10-20% schedule reserve and 5-15% cost reserve depending on uncertainty. Projects without reserves systematically overrun.
