**Manufacturing**:
- Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) = (Availability × Performance × Quality)
- Cycle time: How long product takes through process
- First-pass yield: % of units meeting specs without rework
- Inventory turns: How many times inventory sold per year

**Supply Chain**:
- Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO): How long inventory held
- Order-to-cash cycle time: Time from order to customer payment
- Perfect order rate: % of orders delivered on time, in full, with no damage
- Supply chain cost: % of revenue spent on supply chain

**Service Operations**:
- Utilization: Percentage of time resources are used
- Service level: % of calls answered within target time
- Customer wait time: Average wait before service starts
- First-contact resolution: % of issues resolved without escalation

**Project Management**:
- Schedule variance: Actual progress vs. planned progress
- Cost variance: Actual cost vs. budgeted cost
- Scope change frequency: Number and cost of scope changes
- Stakeholder satisfaction: Satisfaction with project outcomes

**Quality**:
- Defect rate: Number of defects per unit produced
- First-pass yield: % meeting specifications without rework
- Return/warranty rate: % of products returned
- Customer complaints per 1000 units
