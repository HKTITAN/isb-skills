**Definition**: Organizational ability to develop, build, and reconfigure competencies to address rapidly changing environments.

**Core capabilities**:
- Sensing: Ability to perceive market shifts and opportunities
- Seizing: Ability to mobilize resources to capture opportunities
- Transforming: Ability to reconfigure organization and capabilities

**Why important**:
- Static competitive advantage erodes
- Continuous renewal required
- Organizations must evolve faster than market changes
- Capabilities > assets in turbulent markets

#### Sources of Dynamic Capability

**R&D and innovation**: Systematic capability development

**Learning and experimentation**: Building from trials and failures

**Organizational flexibility**: Ability to reconfigure quickly

**Leadership**: Vision and commitment to renewal
