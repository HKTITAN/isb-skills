**Definition**: Partnerships between firms to achieve objectives neither could alone while maintaining independence.

#### Types of Alliances

**Joint Ventures (JVs)**:
- Separate legal entity with shared ownership
- Advantages: Clear governance, dedicated management, liability separation
- Disadvantages: Complex exit, coordination overhead

**Licensing**:
- IP or technology transfer; licensee pays royalty
- Advantages: Capital-light, rapid market entry, risk transfer
- Disadvantages: Limited control, IP risk

**Franchising**:
- Standardized business model replication
- Advantages: Rapid expansion, local knowledge, capital efficiency
- Disadvantages: Brand dilution risk, franchisee quality variance
- Example: McDonald's, Subway restaurant franchises

**Strategic Partnerships**:
- Flexible, often non-equity arrangements
- Advantages: Flexibility, low commitment, rapid adjustment
- Disadvantages: Lower commitment, limited control

**Consortia**:
- Multiple organizations on specific projects or standards
- Advantages: Shared risk, economies of scale, collective bargaining
- Disadvantages: Slow decisions, member conflicts

#### Alliance Governance

**Decision rights**: Who controls strategic and operational decisions?

**Control mechanisms**: Board representation, voting rights, management hierarchy

**Governance modes**:
- Equity JV: Shared ownership, board representation
- Contractual JV: Agreement-based, contract-specified terms
- Minority equity: <50% ownership, board seat or veto rights

**Key trade-off**: Higher control (equity ownership) requires higher investment but reduces opportunism risk

#### Partner Selection

**Strategic fit**: Complementary capabilities, aligned objectives

**Organizational fit**: Compatible management styles, cultures, decision-making speeds

**Financial stability**: Strong balance sheets, realistic return expectations

**Track record**: History of successful partnerships, industry reputation

#### Alliance Success Factors

**Trust mechanisms**:
- **Contractual trust**: Clear terms, explicit metrics, enforcement mechanisms
- **Relational trust**: Regular communication, transparency, shared problem-solving
- **Swift trust**: Demonstrated competence, initial commitments honored

**Common failure points**:
- Structural: Misaligned incentives, unclear metrics
- Relational: Trust breakdown, communication failure
- Operational: Integration difficulties, execution failures

#### Portfolio Management

Successful firms manage multiple alliances simultaneously
- Balance breadth (many alliances) with depth (intensive partnerships)
- Mix new, developing, and mature alliances
- Learn from alliance experiences across organization
