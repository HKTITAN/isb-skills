**Definition**: A defensible competitive position that allows returns above cost of capital over years.

#### Types of Advantages

**Cost advantage**:
- Produce at lower cost than competitors
- Defend through operational discipline, scale economies, proprietary processes
- Sustain through continuous efficiency improvement

**Differentiation advantage**:
- Unique value customers value and willing to pay premium for
- Defend through brand loyalty, switching costs, ecosystem lock-in
- Sustain through continuous innovation

**Focus advantage**:
- Serve specific niche better than generalists
- Defend through specialized capabilities, deep customer knowledge
- Sustain through focus and expertise

#### Barriers to Imitation

**Structural barriers**:
- Scale economies
- Capital requirements
- Network effects
- Switching costs
- Regulatory constraints
- Brand and reputation

**Resource-based barriers**:
- Proprietary technology
- Proprietary data
- Unique talent
- Accumulated know-how

**Behavioral barriers**:
- Cost leadership discipline
- Relationship stickiness
- First-mover advantage
- Credible commitment to fight competition

#### Sustainability Framework

**How long will advantage last?**
1. How easily can competitors copy?
2. What are the barriers to imitation?
3. How much investment is required to match?
4. How quickly does the industry innovate?

**What is required to maintain advantage?**
1. Continuous investment in the source (R&D, brand building)
2. Building organizational capabilities
3. Creating switching costs
4. Evolving faster than competitors
