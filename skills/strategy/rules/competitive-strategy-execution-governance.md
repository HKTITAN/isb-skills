**Definition**: How strategy is implemented, monitored, and adjusted.

#### Balanced Scorecard

Framework for monitoring and adjusting strategy:

**Financial perspective**: Revenue growth, profitability, return on capital

**Customer perspective**: Satisfaction, retention, market share, brand

**Internal processes**: Quality, efficiency, cycle time, innovation

**Learning and growth**: Employee development, capability building, organizational health

**Benefits**:
- Balanced view of performance (not just financials)
- Links strategy to execution
- Enables course correction
- Creates accountability

#### Strategy Review Cadence

**Quarterly**: Operational metrics and performance against plan
- Are we executing?
- Do we need tactical adjustments?

**Annual**: Full strategy review against market changes
- Is market landscape changing?
- Do we need strategic adjustments?
- Are scenario indicators changing?

**Every 3-5 years**: Fundamental strategy reassessment
- Is industry structure changing?
- Are competitive dynamics shifting?
- Do we need different strategic direction?
