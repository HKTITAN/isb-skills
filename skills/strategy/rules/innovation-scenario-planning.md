**Definition**: Developing multiple plausible futures and designing strategies robust across scenarios or adaptive to different outcomes.

#### Why Scenario Planning Matters

**Limitations of traditional forecasting**:
- Assumes historical patterns continue
- Predicts single "most likely" future
- Often overconfident and wrong
- Doesn't prepare for discontinuous change

**Scenario planning strengths**:
- Expands thinking about possibilities
- Identifies key uncertainties and drivers
- Develops strategies robust across multiple futures
- Creates organizational preparedness
- Improves decision quality

#### Uncertainty Analysis

**Sources of uncertainty**:

**Measurement uncertainty**: 
- Incomplete information about current state
- Data quality limitations

**Structural uncertainty**:
- Relationships between variables poorly understood
- Behavioral responses unpredictable

**Parameter uncertainty**:
- Known relationships; unknown values
- Can be modeled with ranges

**Recognized ignorance**:
- Unknown unknowns; events outside model
- Black swans; rare but high-impact events

#### Building Scenarios

**Step 1: Identify key uncertainties**
- What is most uncertain about the future?
- What could have biggest impact on strategy?

**Step 2: Identify driving forces**
- What factors drive the uncertainties?
- Create scenario framework (2x2 matrix with two key uncertainties)

**Step 3: Develop scenario narratives**
- Tell coherent story for each scenario
- Explain how it comes to be
- Detail what world looks like

**Step 4: Test strategy against scenarios**
- How does strategy perform in each scenario?
- Identify vulnerable scenarios

#### Adaptive Strategy Design

**Robust strategy**:
- Performs acceptably across most scenarios
- Doesn't depend on single scenario outcome
- Hedged approach; no extreme bets
- Requires less perfect foresight

**Flexible strategy**:
- Allows adaptation as scenarios unfold
- Quick pivoting; low sunk costs
- Preserves optionality

**Win-and-learn**:
- Invest in early indicators of scenario
- Prepare contingency plans
- Learn from signals; trigger strategy reset

**Real options perspective**:
- Waiting option: Defer decision; gather more information
- Expansion option: Start small; expand if scenario favorable
- Contraction option: Scale back if scenario unfolds unfavorably
- Switching option: Pivot strategy if scenario changes
- Abandonment option: Exit if conditions deteriorate
