**Stable Matching and Gale-Shapley Algorithm**
- Problem: N men and N women with preferences; find stable matching (no pair prefers each other to current matches)
- Algorithm: Iterative proposal-response until all matched
- Property: Algorithm produces stable outcome; proposers do better on average than reactors
- Applications: Medical residency, school choice (Boston mechanism), kidney exchange, labor markets

**Chicken-and-Egg Problem in Two-Sided Markets**
- Network effect: Value increases with participation on both sides
- Coordination failure: Need both sides for critical mass; each side needs the other
- Solutions: Subsidize one side (Uber drivers), scarcity positioning (luxury goods), focused niches (industry-specific platforms)
- Timing: Subsidies often front-loaded; recoup through other side later

**Platform Governance Rules**
- Participation: Open (maximize supply) vs. curated (ensure quality)
- Pricing: Platform-set, market-determined, or negotiated
- Quality assurance: Reputation systems, verification, guarantees
- Dispute resolution: Platform arbitration affects both sides' participation willingness
- Data access: Privacy concerns vs. platform insights for better matching
