**Entry Economics**
- Hurdle rate: Return required to justify entry investment
  - Must exceed cost of capital plus risk premium
- Post-entry competition: Entry-reducing competition; long-run equilibrium may sustain zero economic profit
- Barriers to entry: Economies of scale, switching costs, brand loyalty, patents, capital requirements, regulation
- Strategic entry: Enter if can sustain competitive advantage; otherwise unprofitable long-term

**Exit Decisions**
- Sunk costs: Costs that cannot be recovered; should not affect exit decision
- Exit rule: Exit if price < average variable cost; continue if covers variable costs despite losses
- Strategic holding: Even at losses, may hold position if valuable option value (waiting for better times)
- Market discipline: Unprofitable firms eventually exit; asset value in alternative uses determines timing
