**5 Forces Analysis (Porter)**
- Competitive intensity from: Rivalry among competitors, bargaining power of suppliers, bargaining power of buyers, threat of substitutes, threat of entry
- Economic analysis: Each force reflects market structure; determines pricing power and profitability
- Elasticity lens: Supplier/buyer power determined by elasticity of supply/demand and availability of alternatives

**Value Chain Analysis**
- Activity-level analysis: Identify which activities create value and capture value
- Cost structure: Understand variable costs (scale economies), fixed costs (barriers to entry)
- Positioning: Differentiate in high-value activities; achieve cost advantage in cost-sensitive activities

**Game Theory Applied to Competition**
- Strategic interdependence: Competitor actions affect your outcomes; account for likely competitor responses
- Prisoner's dilemma: Individually rational behavior (price cuts) may lead to collectively bad outcomes
- Commitment: Credible commitments (visible investments, public statements) shape competitor expectations

**Pricing Framework**
- Value-based pricing: Set price based on customer willingness to pay (demand-driven)
- Cost-plus pricing: Set price as cost plus markup (cost-driven); less sophisticated but simpler
- Competitive pricing: Set price based on competitor prices; accounts for market structure
- Dynamic pricing: Adjust prices based on real-time demand (requires demand information)
