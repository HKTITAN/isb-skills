**Consumer Surplus**
- Definition: Difference between willingness to pay (WTP) and actual price paid
- Calculation: Integral of demand curve above price; area under demand, above price line
- Visualization: Triangle in linear demand; base = quantity, height = (intercept - price)
- Effect of price decrease: Consumer surplus increases (pay less for same quantity, buy more)
- Market efficiency: Perfect competition maximizes total consumer surplus

**Producer Surplus**
- Definition: Difference between price received and willingness to supply (minimum acceptable price)
- Calculation: Area above supply curve, below price line
- Visualization: Triangle in linear supply; base = quantity, height = (price - intercept)
- Effect of price increase: Producer surplus increases
- Market efficiency: Perfect competition maximizes total producer surplus

**Total Surplus and Deadweight Loss**
- Total surplus: Consumer surplus + producer surplus; measure of market efficiency
- Allocative efficiency: Perfect competition maximizes total surplus at quantity where P = MC
- Deadweight loss: Lost surplus from units not traded that would create value
  - Monopoly: Restricts output below competitive level; creates deadweight loss
  - Taxation: Creates wedge between buyer and seller prices; units not traded are deadweight loss
  - Regulation: Price ceilings, floors create deadweight loss
