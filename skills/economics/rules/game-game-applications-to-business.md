**Auction Theory**
- English auction: Ascending bid; bidders observe other bids; highest bidder wins at own price
- Dutch auction: Descending bid; first bidder at announced price wins
- First-price sealed-bid: Bidders submit hidden bids; highest wins at own bid
- Second-price sealed-bid (Vickrey): Highest bidder wins but pays second-highest bid
  - Strategic insight: Bidding true value optimal (weakly dominant strategy)
  - Property: Truthful bidding incentivized; highest valuation always wins
  - Efficiency: Allocative efficiency despite sealed bids

**Strategic Pricing**
- Airline pricing: Firms carefully observe competitor prices; dynamic pricing based on demand
- Price wars: Can result in prisoner's dilemma; both firms cut prices, hurting profitability
- Collusion: Joint profit maximization requires coordination and enforcement (often illegal)
- Retaliation: Price competition can become fierce if firms view as aggressive move meriting response
