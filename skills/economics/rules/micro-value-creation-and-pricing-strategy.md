**Value Creation vs. Value Capture**
- Value creation: Transactions increase total wealth; customer WTP > production cost
- Value capture: Division of created value between customer (consumer surplus) and firm (profit)
- Strategic challenge: Create value AND capture it; innovations create value but capture little if easily copied
- Trade-off: Lower price captures less value for firm but creates more consumer surplus

**Price Discrimination**
- Objective: Charge different prices to different customers based on willingness to pay
- Perfect price discrimination: Charge each customer maximum they're willing to pay
  - Effect: Eliminates consumer surplus; captures all value
  - Impossible in practice but provides benchmark

**Price Discrimination Strategies**
- Versioning: Different product versions for different segments
  - Example: Software lite vs. enterprise; airlines basic vs. premium economy
- Geographic pricing: Prices vary by region based on local demand/competition
  - Example: Video games priced higher in wealthy countries
- Time-based pricing: Price varies by time period
  - Example: Peak vs. off-peak pricing; advance purchase discounts
- Quantity discounts: Lower per-unit price for larger purchases
  - Wholesale pricing, bulk discounts

**Competitive Pricing Dynamics**
- Homogeneous products: Prices converge to marginal cost; fierce competition; low rents
- Differentiated products: P > MC sustainable; stronger differentiation → higher pricing power
- Skim pricing: High initial price targeting early adopters; risk of inviting competition
- Penetration pricing: Low initial price for volume; locks in customers; limits future price increases
- Dynamic pricing: Real-time adjustment based on demand (airlines, ride-sharing, electricity)
