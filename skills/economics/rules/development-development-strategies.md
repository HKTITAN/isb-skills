**Export-Led vs. Import Substitution**
- Export-led: Integrate with global markets; specialize in comparative advantage; South Korea, Vietnam success
- Import substitution: Protect domestic industry; develop local champions; Latin America, India relative failure
- Success factors: Export-led benefits from competition discipline, technology transfer, market access
- Failure factors: Import substitution protection becomes permanent; inefficient firms never mature

**Development Challenges**
- Climate vulnerability: Developing countries most exposed (agriculture-dependent, low adaptive capacity)
- Inequality: Rises in early development (Kuznets curve); controlled through education, taxation, safety nets
- Brain drain: Talented workers emigrate; host country loses investment and productivity growth
- Resource curse: Natural resource wealth can reduce incentive for institution-building; volatile revenues
