**Stable Marriage Problem**
- Problem: Matching two groups (men and women, students and schools, workers and firms)
- Stability: No pair prefers each other to current matches (no blocking pair)
- Deferred acceptance algorithm: (Gale-Shapley) guarantees stable matching
  - Process: Men propose in order; women accept if better than current option
  - Property: Stable outcome, strategy-proof for proposing side
  - Application: Medical residency matching, school choice, kidney exchange

**Two-Sided Platform Markets**
- Characteristics: Value depends on both sides (users and creators, buyers and sellers)
- Network effects: More users increase value for creators; more creators increase value for users
- Chicken-and-egg problem: Platform needs critical mass on both sides; hard to bootstrap
- Solutions: Subsidize one side initially, cross-subsidization, exclusive content, switching costs

**Network Effects and Thickness**
- Positive feedback: More participants increase value, attracting more participants
- Lock-in: Large installed base creates high switching costs for users
- Winner-take-most: Markets with strong network effects often dominated by single platform
- Examples: Facebook, eBay, Uber, Airbnb
