**Expected Value**
- Definition: Sum of (probability × outcome) for all possible outcomes
- Application: Evaluate risky decisions; higher expected value generally preferred
- Limitation: Ignores risk; two options with same expected value but different variability are not equivalent
- Risk premium: Individuals often willing to pay to reduce risk (insurance, diversification)

**Risk and Variance**
- Variance: Measure of dispersion; how much outcomes vary around expected value
- Standard deviation: Square root of variance; same units as original variable
- Risk aversion: Most individuals dislike risk; willing to sacrifice expected value to reduce variance
- Risk tolerance: Ability and willingness to bear risk varies by individual and situation

**Value of Information**
- Definition: Willingness to pay for information that reduces uncertainty
- Perfect information: Eliminates uncertainty; value = expected value with info minus expected value without
- Imperfect information: Reduces but doesn't eliminate uncertainty; value less than perfect information
- Search costs: Value of additional information must exceed cost of obtaining it
