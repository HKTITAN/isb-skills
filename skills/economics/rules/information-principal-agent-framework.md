**Setup and Agency Costs**
- Principal (shareholder, customer) hires Agent (CEO, contractor)
- Agent's effort affects both parties' payoffs, but effort is hidden (moral hazard) or information hidden
- Agency costs arise from:
  1. Monitoring costs: Principal pays for supervision, audits, incentive contracts
  2. Bonding costs: Agent pays for assurance (insurance, guarantee)
  3. Residual loss: Remaining divergence after monitoring and bonding
- Total agency cost = monitoring + bonding + residual loss

**Solutions to Agency Problems**
1. Incentive alignment: Pay based on outcomes (bonus, equity, commission)
   - Tradeoff: Creates risk for agent; agent demands higher expected pay
2. Monitoring and governance: Board oversight, audits, KPI dashboards
   - Costly, imperfect, limited to observable measures
3. Long-term relationship: Repeated interaction, threat of dismissal
   - Effective when reputation at stake; fails with one-time transactions
4. Market competition: Multiple agents compete, worst performers exit
   - Requires comparability and exit costs
5. Legal/contractual enforcement: Bonding, liability, specific performance
   - Limited to verifiable, enforceable behaviors
