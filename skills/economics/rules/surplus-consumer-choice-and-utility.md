**Indifference Curves**
- Definition: Combinations of two goods that provide equal utility
- Properties: Downward-sloping, convex to origin, don't cross
- Marginal rate of substitution (MRS): Slope; quantity of Y willing to give up for one more unit of X
- Diminishing MRS: Willing to give up less Y as consume more X (increasing marginal value of X)

**Budget Constraint**
- Definition: Spending = Px·X + Py·Y; constraint on affordable combinations
- Slope: -Px/Py (relative price)
- Shifts: Income changes shift parallel; price changes change slope

**Optimal Choice**
- Condition: Indifference curve tangent to budget constraint (MRS = Px/Py)
- Interpretation: Marginal utility ratio equals price ratio; can't improve by trading
- Response to price change: Substitution effect (buy more of cheaper good), income effect (change consumption of all goods)
