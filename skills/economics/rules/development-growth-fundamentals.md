**Solow Growth Model**
- Output decomposition: Growth from capital accumulation (K), labor growth (L), human capital (H), technology (A)
- Steady state: Capital per worker reaches equilibrium where investment = capital depreciation
- Convergence: Conditional convergence (countries converge to own steady state based on institutions, savings, human capital)
- Implication: Technology and institutions determine long-run income; capital accumulation temporary effect

**Poverty Trap Dynamics**
- Mechanism: Low income → Low savings → Low capital → Low productivity → Persist
- Causes: Inability to save, low human capital, poor health, weak institutions, external debt, brain drain
- Big push: Coordinated investment across sectors (infrastructure, education, health) can break trap
- Escape requirements: Political will, education, health, institutional reform, export-led growth
