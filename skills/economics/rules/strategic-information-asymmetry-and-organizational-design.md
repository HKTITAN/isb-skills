**Adverse Selection: The Lemons Problem**
- Market unraveling: Buyers offer average quality price → High-quality sellers withdraw → Market fills with low-quality
- Examples: Used cars, insurance (high-risk overrepresented), credit markets
- Solutions: Signaling (warranties, brand investment), screening (inspections, trials), reputation, certification

**Moral Hazard: Hidden Action After Contract**
- Definition: Protected party takes excessive risk post-contract; insurer/principal can't observe
- Examples: Insurance (less careful after coverage), employment (shirking), banking (excessive risk)
- Solutions: Incentive alignment (bonuses, equity), monitoring (audits), costly signals (skin in game)

**Principal-Agent Framework**
- Conflict: Agent makes decisions affecting principal; interests may diverge
- Agency costs: Monitoring (expensive), bonding (guarantees), residual loss (remaining divergence)
- Solutions: Incentive contracts (stock options), monitoring, long-term relationships, market competition

**Vertical Integration Decision**
- Integrate when: Hold-up problems, information asymmetry, relationship-specific assets, coordination needs
- Don't integrate when: Supplier expertise critical, high capital requirements, suppliers serve many customers
- Real options: Maintain flexibility through partnerships with option to integrate if needed
