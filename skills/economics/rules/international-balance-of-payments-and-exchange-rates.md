**Current and Financial Accounts**
- Current account: Trade balance + income flows + transfers
- Financial account: FDI + portfolio investment + other capital flows
- Identity: Current + Financial = 0 (with measurement errors)
- Sustainability: Persistent deficits require willing foreign investors; loss of confidence creates crisis risk

**Exchange Rate Determination**
- Purchasing Power Parity: Same basket costs same in both currencies; exchange rates adjust
- Deviations: PPP fails short-term; sticky prices, capital flows, risk premiums cause persistent misalignment
- Interest rate parity: Risk-free rate differences equal expected currency appreciation
- Expectations: Expected appreciation causes immediate appreciation through capital flows

**Exchange Rate Regimes**
- Fixed rates: Eliminate uncertainty; require large reserves; limit monetary policy autonomy
- Floating rates: Market-determined; monetary independence; currency volatility
- Managed float: Mostly floating with periodic intervention (current standard)
- Trilemma: Can't simultaneously have fixed rates + free capital flows + independent monetary policy
