**Hidden Information Problems**
- Asymmetric information: One player knows more than other when making decisions
- Signaling: Informed player takes costly action to reveal private information (education signals ability)
- Pooling vs separating: Equilibria where different types reveal (separating) or hide (pooling) information

**Bluffing and Mixed Strategies**
- Mixed strategy equilibrium: Players randomize strategies to prevent exploitation
- Bluffing: In poker, sometimes bet on weak hand to prevent opponents from knowing hand strength
- Unpredictability: Mixing makes player harder to exploit; too predictable strategy is exploitable
