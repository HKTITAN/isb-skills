**Monopolization and Barriers to Entry**
- Legal monopoly: High barriers (patents, regulation, scale, control of inputs) prevent competition
- Predatory behavior: Firm uses position to further restrict entry (exclusive dealing, tie-ins, predatory pricing)
- Welfare effects: Monopoly raises prices, reduces quantity, creates deadweight loss
- Policy response: Antitrust enforcement, rate regulation for natural monopolies

**Merger Analysis**
- Horizontal merger: Competitors combine; raises market concentration
- Concerns: Reduced competition, potential for coordinated behavior, reduced innovation
- Defense: Merger creates efficiencies (cost savings, R&D synergies) outweighing competitive harm
- Vertical merger: Supplier and downstream firm combine; may foreclose rivals from inputs
- Conglomerate merger: Unrelated businesses; less competitive concern
