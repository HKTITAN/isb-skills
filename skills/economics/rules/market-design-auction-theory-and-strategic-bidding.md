**Auction Formats and Revenue Equivalence**
- English (ascending): Price rises; highest wins; transparent; good revenues but reveals valuations
- Dutch (descending): Price falls; first bidder wins at current price; fast but leaves money on table
- First-price sealed-bid: Highest bid wins at own bid; private bids; bid shading (bid below true valuation)
- Second-price sealed-bid (Vickrey): Highest wins but pays second price; truth-telling dominant strategy; rarely used
- Revenue Equivalence Theorem: Under symmetric valuations and risk-neutral bidders, formats generate equivalent revenue; breaks down due to bid shading
- Spectrum auctions: Design matters tremendously; proper mechanism can increase revenue 30-50%

**Quality-Adjusted Auctions**
- Online advertising: Modified second-price mechanics; quality score affects ad placement
- Scoring auctions: Weight multiple dimensions (price, quality, local content); highest score wins
- Application: Government procurement, spectrum licenses, complex B2B markets
