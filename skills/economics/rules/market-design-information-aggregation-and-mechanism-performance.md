**Incentive Compatibility**
- Definition: Mechanism where self-interested behavior aligns with desired outcomes
- Example: Second-price auction makes honest bidding dominant strategy
- Challenge: Mechanisms can incentivize gaming, collusion, strategic behavior

**Strategic Behavior in Mechanisms**
- Bid shading: First-price auctions incentivize underbidding
- Collusion: Competitors coordinate to suppress bids; detected through mechanism variance
- Entry/exit timing: Drivers surge supply at peak times, reducing everyone's earnings (rational response to incentives)
