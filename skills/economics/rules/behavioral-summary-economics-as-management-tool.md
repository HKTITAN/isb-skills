Economics provides frameworks for understanding:
- How customers, competitors, and suppliers make decisions (demand, game theory, information economics)
- How markets allocate resources and determine prices (supply-demand, market structures, competition)
- How policies and institutions shape behavior and outcomes (public finance, development, behavioral)
- How to make better strategic decisions in uncertain environments (decision-making, risk)

Master economic thinking to:
- Anticipate customer and competitor responses to price and strategy changes
- Identify sources of value creation and value capture in your business
- Design pricing, incentives, and organizational structures aligned with economic realities
- Evaluate market opportunities and competitive positioning using economic frameworks
- Understand macroeconomic trends affecting industry growth, costs, and financing
