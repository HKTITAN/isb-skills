**Inclusive vs. Extractive Institutions**
- Inclusive: Broad property rights, open markets, rule of law, low corruption → Sustained growth
- Extractive: Power concentrated, no rule of law, high corruption → Slow growth despite resources
- Historical dependence: Colonial institutions shape modern institutions; difficult to change
- Corruption costs: Each percentage point of corruption reduces growth 0.5-1% annually; compounds vastly

**Human Capital Investment**
- Education returns: Each additional school year increases earnings 10% (private return); broader growth benefits
- Health importance: Mortality reduction precedes income growth; health improvements enable productivity gains
- Female education: Generates largest externalities; reduces fertility, improves child health, increases workforce participation
- Access barriers: Poor families can't afford; public investment necessary for broad human capital development
