**Gross Domestic Product**
- Definition: Total monetary value of final goods/services produced within country in period
- Approaches:
  - Expenditure: GDP = C + I + G + (X - M) (consumption, investment, government, net exports)
  - Income: GDP = wages + profits + interest + rent (total income earned)
  - Production: Sum of value-added at each stage (avoids double-counting)
- Nominal vs real: Nominal uses current prices; real adjusts for inflation
- Per capita: GDP/population; measures average income

**Inflation and Price Indices**
- CPI (Consumer Price Index): Average price change for basket of goods purchased by typical household
- Core inflation: Excludes volatile items (food, energy); measures underlying inflation trend
- Deflator: Implicit price index; ratio of nominal to real GDP
- Measurement issues: Quality improvements, substitution bias (consumers switch to cheaper goods)

**Unemployment**
- Unemployment rate: Percentage of labor force unemployed; labor force = employed + unemployed
- Types:
  - Frictional: Natural job search and matching; always present even at full employment
  - Structural: Mismatch between worker skills and job requirements; regional mismatches
  - Cyclical: Result of business cycle; rises in recessions, falls in expansions
- Natural rate: Full employment level where inflation stable; approximately 4-5% in developed economies
- Underemployment: Working part-time when prefer full-time; not captured in unemployment rate
