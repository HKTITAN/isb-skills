**High retention:** Cohort retention curves flatten at high levels (50%+ month-over-month for consumer, 70%+ for B2B). Repeat usage is the truest signal of value.

**Organic growth:** Referrals and word-of-mouth drive significant portion of new users. Growth outpaces marketing spend.

**Price inelasticity:** Customers don't leave when price rises. They've internalized value.

**Customer enthusiasm:** Users spontaneously mention product in conversations. Feel like evangelists.

**Low churn:** New cohorts retain similarly over time. You're keeping customers, not just acquiring.

**Qualitative feedback:** Consistent customer descriptions of solving painful problems.

**Predictable demand:** Sales pipeline predictable; customers seek you out.
