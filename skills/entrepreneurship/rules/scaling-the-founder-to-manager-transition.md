**The "40-person cliff":**
At ~40 employees, company needs professional management. Founder can no longer know everyone and maintain culture through osmosis. Must delegate and trust others.

**Challenges:**
- Founder identity often tied to "doing"
- Delegation feels like loss of control
- Quality execution may decrease initially
- Managing managers is particularly hard

**Founder team dynamics at scale:**
- Co-founder conflict increases (less joint decision-making, more hierarchical)
- Different founders suited for different phases
- Some founders want to build early; others want to scale later
