Product-market fit (PMF) is when your product satisfies strong market demand. Customers request it before it's available and actively recommend it.

Before PMF: "thrashing"—constantly changing product, messaging, or target because nothing feels right. Growth is slow, churn is high, word-of-mouth is weak.
