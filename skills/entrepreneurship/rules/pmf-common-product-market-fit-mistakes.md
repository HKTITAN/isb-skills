impact: CRITICAL

**Mistaking traction for PMF:** Quick month-1 growth with flat month-2 indicates lack of retention.

**Ignoring churn:** Measuring new signups without retention. High churn destroys growth.

**Solving luxury problem:** Solution is nice but not essential. PMF requires weekly+ need.

**Being in love with solution:** You've built something elegant; customers want something different.

**Premature scaling:** Hire big sales team before retention is clear. Sales acquire customers who churn.
