A startup is a young company founded to solve a problem, serve a market, or explore an unproven business model. It's intentionally different from a traditional business: startups operate under extreme uncertainty with limited capital and burn cash pursuing rapid growth rather than immediate profitability.

**Key startup characteristics:**
- Extreme uncertainty: The business model is unproven
- Limited runway: Months to find sustainable economics
- All-in commitment: Full-time founder involvement required
- Growth-focused: Target 10x-100x expansion, not incremental gains
- High risk/reward: 90%+ fail; winners return 100x-1000x

**Not every new company is a startup.** A family restaurant is a new business; a venture-backed restaurant chain is a startup. The distinction is deliberate pursuit of scaled growth.
