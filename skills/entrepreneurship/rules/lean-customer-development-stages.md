**Stage 1: Problem (0-3 months)**
- Interview 50+ potential customers
- Ask about current solutions and pain points
- Show concept/prototype
- Goal: 40%+ confirm strong pain, 20%+ would switch

**Stage 2: Solution (3-9 months)**
- Build MVP, put in customer hands
- Measure activation and retention
- Iterate based on feedback
- Get 10-20 paying customers
- Goal: 40%+ activation, 30%+ week-4 retention, repeatable sales process

**Stage 3: Scaling (9+ months)**
- Optimize unit economics
- Build scalable sales process
- Plan product expansion
- Hire specialist team
- Goal: LTV:CAC > 3:1, predictable sales, 50+ paying customers
