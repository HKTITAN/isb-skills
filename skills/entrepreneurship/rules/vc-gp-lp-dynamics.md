**GPs (General Partners):** Manage fund, raise capital, identify investments, sit on boards, shepherd toward exits.

**LPs (Limited Partners):** Commit capital but have limited input on investments.

**Management fee:** Typically 2% of fund size annually (covers operations)
- $100M fund charges $2M/year for salaries, office, etc.

**Carried interest:** GPs keep 20% (sometimes 10-30%) of profits from exits
- Aligns GP incentives with LP returns
- Example: Fund invests $5M in Company X. At exit, company worth $300M. Investors' $5M became $15M. GPs keep 20% of $10M profit = $2M. LPs get $8M profit.

**This structure explains VC behavior:**
- Chase big wins (only way to return fund)
- May be aggressive with governance (pressure for growth)
- Lose money on most bets; winners must be huge
