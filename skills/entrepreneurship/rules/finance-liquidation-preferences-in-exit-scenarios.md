How proceeds are distributed when company sells.

**Example (non-participating preference):**
- Company sells for $10M
- Series A invested $5M for 1x non-participating preferred stock (owns 50%)

**Waterfall:**
1. Series A receives $5M (their investment returned)
2. Remaining $5M distributed per ownership percentage
3. Series A: 50% of $5M = $2.5M
4. **Series A total: $7.5M**
5. Founders split remaining $2.5M

**With participating preference (more aggressive):**
1. Series A receives $5M (their investment)
2. Series A ALSO receives 50% of total proceeds = $5M
3. Series A total = $10M (but only $5M exists for others!)
4. Founders get nothing

**Key insight:** Participating preferences are founder-unfavorable. Negotiate for 1x non-participating or lower.
