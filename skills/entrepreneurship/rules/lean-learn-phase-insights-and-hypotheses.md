Extract insights from data to inform next iteration.

**Conversion funnel analysis:**
- Where do users drop off?
- Which features get most engagement?
- What characterizes power users?

**Customer research synthesis:**
- What problem did customers actually reveal?
- What jobs are they trying to accomplish?
- What's their current solution?
- Why would they choose yours?

**Build hypotheses for next iteration:**
- "If we improve onboarding, activation will increase to 40%"
- "Users receiving feature X email have 2x retention"
- "Segment Y is 5x more likely to upgrade than segment Z"

**Key insight:** Learning is the output, not vanity metrics. "We had 1,000 beta signups" means nothing without "Only 5% activated, so onboarding is broken."
