"How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?"

- **Very disappointed (40%+)**: Strong PMF indicator
- **Somewhat disappointed**: Ambiguous; likely not yet at PMF
- **Not disappointed**: Product not compelling; major issues

This reveals whether customers see your product as essential (strong loss aversion) vs. casual interest.
