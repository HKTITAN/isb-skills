**Shift to:**
- Stop changing core features: Your product resonates
- Expand marketing channels: With retention + word-of-mouth, paid marketing becomes efficient
- Hire to scale: Build sales, marketing, operations, engineering teams
- Raise growth capital: Investors back PMF signals
- Expand to adjacent segments: Apply solution to nearby customer types

**How to know you're ready to scale:**
- 30-day retention exceeds targets (40-50% consumer, 70%+ B2B)
- Word-of-mouth accounts for 20%+ of new customers
- CAC < LTV (with conservative estimates)
- Customer feedback consistent: Same problems solved, same benefits cited
- Tested 2-3 customer segments, found clear PMF in one
