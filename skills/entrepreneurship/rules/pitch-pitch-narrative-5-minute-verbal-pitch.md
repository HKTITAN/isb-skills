**Act 1: Problem (1 minute)**
- Paint concrete customer scenario
- Quantify problem impact
- Show customer frustration

**Act 2: Solution (2 minutes)**
- Describe your approach
- Show key benefits
- Tell customer success story

**Act 3: Opportunity (1 minute)**
- Market size and growth rate
- Your target segment
- Market timing ("why now")

**Act 4: Ask (30 seconds)**
- Funding amount
- Use of proceeds
- Call to action
