LBO Economics:
Sources & Uses

Sources:
  Senior Debt (70%):  $700M
  Equity (30%):       $300M
  Total:              $1,000M

Uses:
  Purchase Price:     $1,000M

Year 3 Exit:
Assume EBITDA grows to $150M (from $120M year 1)
Exit at 10x EBITDA: $1,500M

Less: Debt paydown (assume principal repaid): $400M
Equity proceeds: $1,100M

Equity return: $1,100M / $300M = 3.7x
IRR: ~50% (annualized)
