Never rely on single method. Use all three:

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| DCF | Fundamental, intrinsic | Highly sensitive to assumptions |
| Multiples | Market-based, quick | Doesn't reflect unique qualities |
| Asset-Based | Good for real estate | Ignores earning power |

Best practice: Calculate all three, note assumptions, provide valuation range.
