1. **Strategy & Identification:** Define criteria, identify targets
2. **Due Diligence:** Financial, operational, commercial, legal, tax, cultural
3. **Valuation & Negotiation:** Calculate value, determine max price
4. **Regulatory & Approval:** Antitrust review, shareholder votes
5. **Closing & Integration:** Transfer ownership, execute integration
