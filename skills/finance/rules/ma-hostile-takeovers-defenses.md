title: "Hostile Takeovers & Defenses"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: defenses, hostile, takeovers
---

Most M&A is friendly. Hostile occurs when target board rejects; acquirer appeals to shareholders.

Common defenses:
- **Poison pill:** Shareholder rights plan dilutes acquirer's stake
- **Golden parachute:** Expensive severance for management
- **White knight:** Invite friendlier alternative bidder
- **Crown jewels:** Threaten to sell most valuable assets
- **Pac-man:** Target buys acquirer's shares

Modern view: Defenses weakened over time. If target undervalued, shareholders benefit from sale.
