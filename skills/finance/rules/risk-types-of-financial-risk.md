**Market Risk** (price/rate changes):
- Equity price risk
- Interest rate risk
- Commodity price risk
- Currency (FX) risk

**Credit Risk** (counterparty default):
- Default risk
- Downgrade risk

**Liquidity Risk** (can't execute or meet obligations):
- Asset illiquidity
- Funding illiquidity

**Operational Risk** (process failures):
- Fraud, cyber attack, system failure
