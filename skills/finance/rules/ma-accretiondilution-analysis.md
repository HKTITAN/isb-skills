Does the acquisition increase or decrease EPS?

```
Acquirer EPS before: $2.00 (200M earnings, 100M shares)

All-cash deal:
Acquisition price: $500M (paid with debt at 5%, tax rate 25%)
Target net income: $60M
After-tax interest cost: $500M × 5% × 0.75 = $18.75M

Pro forma net income: $200M + $60M - $18.75M = $241.25M
Shares outstanding: 100M (no new shares issued)
New EPS: $241.25M / 100M = $2.41

Accretion: ($2.41 - $2.00) / $2.00 = 20.5% accretive

All-stock deal (issue 40M shares at $50):
Pro forma net income: $200M + $60M = $260M
Shares outstanding: 100M + 40M = 140M
New EPS: $260M / 140M = $1.86

Dilution: ($1.86 - $2.00) / $2.00 = -7% dilutive
```

**Key insight:** Method of financing matters. All-cash (using cheap debt) is accretive. All-stock is dilutive.
