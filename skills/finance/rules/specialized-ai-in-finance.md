**Algorithmic Trading:**
- Identify patterns, execute trades at high speed
- Benefits: Faster execution, consistent discipline
- Risks: Flash crashes, overfitting

**Credit Scoring:**
- ML models predict loan default probability
- Features: Payment history, income, transaction patterns, alternative data
- Risks: Algorithmic bias (trained on biased historical data)

**Fraud Detection:**
- Models detect unusual transaction patterns
- Real-time monitoring vs. batch analysis
- Challenges: Evolving fraud tactics, false positives

**Risk Modeling:**
- AI for loan loss forecasting, market risk
- More data-intensive than traditional methods
- Challenge: Backtesting on limited historical data

**NLP for Financial Analysis:**
- Extract information from earnings calls, news, research
- Sentiment analysis → Market prediction
- Document analysis (contracts, filings) for compliance
