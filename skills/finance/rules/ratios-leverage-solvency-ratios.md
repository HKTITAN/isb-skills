**Debt-to-Equity Ratio** = Total Debt / Shareholders' Equity

Shows capital structure; proportion of debt to equity.

Example:
```
Total Debt: $200M, Equity: $300M
D/E = 0.67 (meaning 40% debt-financed, 60% equity-financed)

Healthy: <1.0 for most industries
Banks normal: 8-10x (by design)
Tech companies: <0.3
Utilities: 1-1.5
```

**Interest Coverage Ratio** = EBIT / Interest Expense

Can the company service debt from operations?

Example:
```
EBIT: $50M, Interest Expense: $10M
Coverage = 5x (operating earnings cover interest 5 times)

Safe: >5x
Adequate: 2-5x
Risky: <2x
Distressed: <1x (cannot cover interest from operations)
```

**EBITDA-to-Debt Ratio** = EBITDA / Total Debt

Years required to pay off debt from earnings.

```
EBITDA: $100M, Total Debt: $250M
Ratio = 0.4x (can pay off debt in 2.5 years)

Healthy: <3.0x
High leverage: >5.0x
```
