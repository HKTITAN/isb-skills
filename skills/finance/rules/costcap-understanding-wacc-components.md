WACC is the company's required return; it must earn WACC minimum on capital to satisfy investors.

**Cost of Equity > Cost of Debt** because:
- Equity is riskier (residual claim after debt holders paid)
- Equity is junior in bankruptcy
- Debt is tax-deductible, lowering its effective cost

**WACC varies by:**
- **Business risk** (cyclical industries higher WACC)
- **Financial risk** (leverage increases WACC)
- **Company size** (small companies higher WACC)
- **Growth stage** (early-stage higher WACC)
