title: "Pitfalls to Avoid"
impact: CRITICAL
impactDescription: "Core MBA framework, foundational to domain"
tags: avoid, pitfalls, technology
---

1. **Focusing only on bottom line:** Net Income ≠ Cash. Always check Operating Cash Flow.

2. **Ignoring accounting policies:** Notes to statements contain critical information about depreciation, revenue recognition, accounting estimates.

3. **Comparing across standards:** IFRS vs. US GAAP differences matter; normalize before comparing.

4. **Treating balance sheet as valuation:** Historical cost ≠ Market value. Inventory may be understated; fixed assets may be obsolete.

5. **Over-relying on single ratio:** Never analyze in isolation. Compare to history, competitors, industry.

6. **Overly optimistic growth:** Perpetual high growth unrealistic. Use GDP-like rates (2-3%) for terminal growth.

7. **Terminal value dominating:** If terminal value is 80%+ of total value, small assumption changes cause large value swings. Sanity-check against comparable multiples.

8. **Wrong discount rate:** WACC should match growth assumptions. Don't use perpetually low WACC if forecasting turnaround.

9. **Ignoring cyclicality:** For cyclical businesses, use normalized or mid-cycle assumptions, not single-year data.

10. **Forgetting net debt adjustment:** DCF values equity, not enterprise value. Always subtract net debt.
