Companies prefer financing hierarchy:
1. **Retained earnings** (no transaction costs, no information requirements)
2. **Debt** (cheaper than equity, less signaling)
3. **Equity** (last resort; signals overvaluation)

Explains why:
- Profitable companies have lower leverage (less need for external capital)
- Leverage increases when companies need cash (acquisitions, growth)
- Equity issuance is rare (signals distress or overvaluation)
