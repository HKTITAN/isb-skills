Optimal capital structure balances benefits and costs of leverage.

**Benefits of Debt:**
- Tax shield (interest deductible)
- Financial discipline (must generate cash to pay interest)
- Lower cost than equity

**Costs of Debt:**
- Financial distress risk (high leverage → bankruptcy risk)
- Reduced financial flexibility (covenants restrict actions)
- Agency costs (creditors monitor, restrict risk-taking)

Typical optimal leverage:
```
Debt-to-Equity: 0.5 to 1.5 (50-60% debt-financed)
Or Debt-to-Assets: 30-50%

Varies by industry and business risk
```
