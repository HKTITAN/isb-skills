**Return on Assets (ROA)** = Net Income / Average Total Assets

Measures how efficiently management generates profit from assets.

Example:
```
Company: Net Income $100M, Average Total Assets $500M
ROA = 100 / 500 = 20%
For every $100 of assets, the company generates $20 of profit
```

Interpretation:
- Varies by industry: Software 25%+, Manufacturing 8-12%, Banks 0.8-1.5%
- Formula: ROA = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover
- Higher is better; reflects efficient asset deployment

**Return on Equity (ROE)** = Net Income / Average Shareholders' Equity

Most important to equity investors; their return on capital.

Example:
```
Company: Net Income $100M, Average Equity $250M
ROE = 100 / 250 = 40%
Shareholders received 40% return on their invested capital
```

Decomposition:
```
ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
    = (NI/Revenue) × (Revenue/Assets) × (Assets/Equity)
```

**Return on Invested Capital (ROIC)** = NOPAT / Invested Capital

Measures return on ALL capital (debt + equity).

```
NOPAT = Operating Income × (1 - Tax Rate)
Invested Capital = Total Assets - Non-interest Bearing Current Liabilities
                 = Debt + Equity

Example:
Operating Income: $100M, Tax Rate: 25%, Invested Capital: $400M
ROIC = $100M × 0.75 / $400M = 18.75%
```

Key insight: **ROIC must exceed WACC to create value**. If ROIC (18.75%) > WACC (10%), company creates shareholder value.
