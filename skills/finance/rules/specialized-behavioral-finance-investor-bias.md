**Key concepts:**

Overconfidence: Investors overestimate accuracy of forecasts.

Anchoring: Over-reliance on initial price points; slow to adjust.

Momentum: Trend-following; chasing performance rather than fundamentals.

Loss aversion: Fear of losses > pleasure from equivalent gains.

Herding: Following crowd rather than independent analysis.

**Implication for valuation:** Markets can misprice securities. Disciplined fundamental analysis can identify opportunities.
