**Growth (Scale vs. Scope):**
- Scope: Enter new markets, products, geographies
- Scale: Consolidation in existing market

**Synergies:**

Revenue Synergies (harder to realize):
```
Cross-selling: Acquirer's sales force sells Target's products
Market expansion: Leverage acquired distribution network
Elimination of competitive overlap: Avoid redundant marketing
Example: $200M revenue target, 10% cross-sell success = $20M incremental revenue
```

Cost Synergies (more reliable):
```
Eliminate duplicate functions (HR, Finance, IT)
Procurement savings (consolidated buying power)
Facility consolidation (close redundant locations)
SG&A reduction (overhead compression)
Example: Combined G&A: $100M, can run on $80M = $20M savings
```

Financial Synergies:
```
Tax benefits (loss carryforwards, R&D credits)
Debt capacity increase (larger company supports more leverage)
Cost of capital reduction (diversified company lower WACC)
```

**Financial Engineering:**
- Leverage benefits (LBO structure amplifies equity returns)
- Valuation arbitrage (buy cheap, improve operations)
- Timing arbitrage (buy in downturn, sell in recovery)
