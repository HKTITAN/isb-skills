Typical timeline:
- Year 1: 25-30% realized (integration takes time)
- Year 2: 60-70% realized
- Year 3+: 90-100% realized (if achieved at all)

Common failures:
- Revenue synergies don't materialize (cross-selling harder than expected)
- Cost synergies overestimated (70% realization typical)
- Key talent leaves (uncertainty, loss of autonomy)
- Systems integration delayed (more expensive than expected)
- Brand damage from aggressive cost-cutting

Best practice: Conservative synergy estimates (apply 30-50% discount to claimed), transparent tracking, willingness to adjust.
