- **Sustainable Growth** = ROE × Retention Ratio = ROE × (1 - Dividend Payout Ratio)
- **Break-even Volume** = Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin
- **Contribution Margin** = Price - Variable Cost per Unit
- **Contribution Margin Ratio** = Contribution Margin / Price
