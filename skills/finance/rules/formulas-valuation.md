- **Enterprise Value** = Market Cap - Cash + Total Debt
- **WACC** = (E/V) × Re + (D/V) × Rd × (1 - Tax Rate)
- **Cost of Equity (CAPM)** = Rf + β × (Rm - Rf)
- **Terminal Value (Gordon)** = Final Year FCF × (1 + g) / (WACC - g)
- **DCF Enterprise Value** = Σ[FCF_t / (1+WACC)^t] + Terminal Value / (1+WACC)^n
- **FCF** = Operating Cash Flow - CapEx
- **P/E** = Stock Price / EPS
- **EV/EBITDA** = (Market Cap - Cash + Debt) / EBITDA
- **Price-to-Book** = Market Cap / Shareholders' Equity
