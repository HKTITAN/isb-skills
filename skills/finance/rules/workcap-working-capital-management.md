**Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)** = DSO + DIO - DPO

Every 1-day reduction in CCC, a company with $10M daily cash burn frees $10M.

**Working Capital Optimization:**

**Receivables:** Accelerate collections
```
Early payment discounts (2/10 net 30): Customer pays 2% less if pays in 10 days
Supply chain financing: Suppliers sell AR at discount for immediate cash
Lockbox services: Bank collects payments directly
Factoring: Sell AR at discount for cash
```

**Inventory:** Reduce days outstanding
```
Just-in-time procurement: Minimize inventory
Demand forecasting: Accurate projections reduce safety stock
Vendor-managed inventory: Supplier manages inventory at customer site
```

**Payables:** Extend payment terms
```
Negotiate longer payment terms with suppliers
But: Must maintain supplier relationships; poor payables damage credibility
```

**EOQ Model (Economic Order Quantity):**

```
EOQ = √(2 × D × O / H)

Where:
D = Annual demand
O = Order cost per order
H = Holding cost per unit per year

Example: D = 10,000 units, O = $100/order, H = $2/unit/year
EOQ = √(2 × 10,000 × 100 / 2) = √1,000,000 = 1,000 units

Order 10 times per year to minimize total costs
```
