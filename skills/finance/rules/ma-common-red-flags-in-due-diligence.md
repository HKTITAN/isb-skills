---
title: "Common Red Flags in Due Diligence"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: common, diligence, due, flags, marketing, operations, quantitative, red
---

- Revenue growing faster than DSO decreasing (customers not actually paying)
- Inventory building up (demand weakness or overstocking)
- One-time items, "normalized" EBITDA (watch for recurring nature)
- Key customer concentration (revenue risk)
- Litigation (contingent liabilities)
- Related-party transactions (quality of earnings questions)
- Loose controls (integration risk)
