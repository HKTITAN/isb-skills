Banks don't work well for DCF (FCFF poorly defined). Use different metrics:

**Return on Assets (ROA)** = Net Income / Total Assets
- Typical: 0.8-1.5%
- Higher for profitable banks

**Return on Equity (ROE)** = Net Income / Average Equity
- Typical: 12-18%
- Constrained by regulatory capital requirements

**Net Interest Margin (NIM)** = (Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Earning Assets
- Typical: 2-4%
- Reflects pricing power and credit risk

**Loan Loss Reserves / Total Loans**
- Conservative underwriting vs. expected deterioration
- Rising reserves = Red flag

**Price-to-Book (P/B) Valuation** (most common for banks)
- Banks typically trade 1.0-2.5x book value
- P/B > 1.5x: Market expects strong returns
- P/B < 1.0: Market expects losses or asset quality issues
