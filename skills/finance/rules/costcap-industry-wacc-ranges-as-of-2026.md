```
Utilities (stable, capital-intensive): 6-8%
Pharmaceuticals (stable cash flows): 7-9%
Banks (regulated, leveraged): 7-10%
Manufacturing (cyclical): 8-10%
Tech/Software (growth, volatile): 9-12%
Retail (competitive): 8-11%
Airlines (cyclical, capital-intensive): 10-14%
Startups/Growth companies: 12-20%+
```

Actual WACC should reflect company-specific factors (management quality, competitive position, geographic risk).
