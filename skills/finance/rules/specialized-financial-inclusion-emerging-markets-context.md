**Microfinance:**
```
Small loans ($200-500) to low-income borrowers
Interest rates: 20-30% (due to operational costs, risk)
Profit margins: Often thin or negative (social mission)
India: Microfinance reached 40M+ borrowers
```

**Mobile Money (India/Africa):**
- UPI (India): Unified Payment Interface, instant digital payments
- M-Pesa (Kenya): SMS-based mobile money
- Enables payments without bank account
- Risks: Fraud, regulatory compliance, agent network

**Jan Dhan-UPI-Aadhaar Trinity (India):**
```
Jan Dhan: 400M+ government bank accounts
Aadhaar: Biometric identity, >99% population
UPI: Instant digital payment infrastructure

Combined effect:
- Enabled cashless, inclusive financial ecosystem
- Reduced corruption, enabled GST, enabled targeted transfers
- Created unprecedented data availability
```
