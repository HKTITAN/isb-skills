**"2 and 20":**
- 2% annual management fee
- 20% of profits (carried interest)

Example fund:
```
$1B fund, 5 companies, 3-year average hold
Assume 3x return on invested capital

Gross value: $1B × 3 = $3B
Less fees (2% × 7 years): ~$140M
Less operating costs: ~$160M
Net gains: $2.7B

GP's 20% carry: $540M
LP's net return: ($3B - $140M - $160M - $540M) / $1B = 2.16x (13% IRR net)
```
