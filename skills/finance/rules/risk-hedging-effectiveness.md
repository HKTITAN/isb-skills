**Perfect hedge:** Derivative payoff exactly offsets underlying exposure.

```
Gold miner will produce 10,000 oz in 6 months
Sell 10,000 oz futures at $1,950/oz
If actual price $1,800: Futures gain = $1,500,000
Revenue locked at $19.5M regardless of actual price
```

**Net hedge:** Hedges portion, retains some upside.

**Cross-hedge:** Use correlated asset when perfect hedge unavailable.
