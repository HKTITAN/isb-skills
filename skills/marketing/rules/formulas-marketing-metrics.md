**Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):**
ROAS = Revenue from Ads / Ad Spend

$4,000 revenue from $1,000 ad spend = 4:1 ROAS (for every $1 spent, $4 revenue)

**Cost Per Lead (CPL):**
CPL = Marketing Spend / Number of Leads Generated

$10,000 spend generating 100 leads = $100 CPL

**Conversion Rate:**
Conversion Rate = (Conversions / Visitors) × 100

100 conversions from 10,000 visitors = 1% conversion rate

**Click-Through Rate (CTR):**
CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100

1,000 clicks from 100,000 ad impressions = 1% CTR

**Net Promoter Score (NPS):**
NPS = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6)

Ranges from -100 to +100. >50 is excellent, 0-50 is good, <0 is concerning
