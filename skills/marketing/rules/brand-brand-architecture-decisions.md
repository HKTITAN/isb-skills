How do you organize multiple products/brands in your portfolio?

**House of Brands**: Independent branded products with distinct positioning
- Example: Procter & Gamble's Tide, Crest, Dawn as separate brands
- Pros: Each can have distinct positioning; failure in one doesn't damage others
- Cons: High marketing costs; can't leverage parent equity
- Best for: Diverse customer segments with different needs

**Branded House**: Single brand with multiple products under same name
- Example: Apple, Virgin, Microsoft
- Pros: Leverage brand equity across portfolio; efficient marketing
- Cons: Risk that one product failure damages entire brand; requires careful extension
- Best for: Consistent positioning across products; strong brand reputation

**Endorsed Brands**: Independent brands with corporate endorsement
- Example: Nescafé endorsed by Nestlé; Courtyard by Marriott
- Pros: Balances autonomy with corporate credibility
- Cons: Dual brand management complexity
- Best for: Extending into new categories while protecting core brand

**Sub-brands**: Secondary brand supporting primary brand
- Example: BMW 3 Series, MacBook Pro
- Pros: Clarifies positioning; manages line extensions
- Cons: Complexity
- Best for: Different product lines needing differentiation
