## title: "SERVQUAL Model"
impact: MEDIUM
impactDescription: "Supporting concept that enhances domain understanding"
tags: frameworks, marketing, model, operations, servqual

Five dimensions customers use to evaluate service quality:

1. **Tangibles**: Physical environment, equipment, staff appearance
2. **Reliability**: Delivers promised service accurately and consistently
3. **Responsiveness**: Willingness to help; prompt service
4. **Assurance**: Staff knowledge, courtesy, conveying trust
5. **Empathy**: Individualized attention, understanding customer needs

Measurement: Surveys rate service on each dimension. Gaps between expectations and perception indicate improvement opportunities.
