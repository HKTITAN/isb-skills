Marketers use multiple measurement approaches:

**Quantitative metrics:**
- Brand awareness: Aided and unaided recall, recognition scores, top-of-mind percentage
- Perceived quality: Survey ratings on quality, reliability, durability
- Brand associations: Strength and frequency of linked attributes (Nike = athletic excellence)
- Loyalty metrics: Repeat purchase rate, customer lifetime value, Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Price premium: What premium percentage can brand command vs. private label?
- Brand valuation: Financial value using revenue multiples, cost-based approaches, or market-based approaches

**Qualitative insights:**
- Brand audits: How is brand perceived? What associations exist?
- Focus groups: Emotional connections, perceived strengths/weaknesses
- Customer interviews: What does brand mean to you? Would you recommend?
- Social listening: Sentiment analysis, brand mentions, word-of-mouth themes
