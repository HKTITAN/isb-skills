Partner with influencers (large engaged followings) to promote product to their audience.

Types by size:
- Mega (1M+): Expensive, broad reach, lower engagement
- Macro (100K-1M): Expensive, good reach, decent engagement
- Micro (10K-100K): Affordable, highly engaged, authentic
- Nano (<10K): Very affordable, extremely engaged, personal

When to use:
- Building awareness in niche
- Product launches
- Fashion, beauty, fitness (established influencer categories)

Economics:
- Mega: $5K-$100K+ per post
- Micro: $100-$1K per post
- Performance-based: Lower upfront, higher if successful
- ROI hard to measure

Best practices:
- Choose influencers with audience alignment
- Prioritize engagement over follower count
- Give creative freedom (authentic performs better)
- Use unique discount codes for tracking
- Start with micro-influencers for testing
- Build long-term relationships
