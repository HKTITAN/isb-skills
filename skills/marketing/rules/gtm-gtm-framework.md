1. **Define target customer**: Who will we serve first?
   - Identify primary target segment
   - Understand their needs and decision process
   - Map decision-making unit (for B2B)
   - Assess addressable market size

2. **Develop positioning and value proposition**: How are we different and better?
   - Points of parity: Table stakes (what we must have)
   - Points of difference: Unique value (what makes us different)
   - Key message: Compelling reason to choose us

3. **Set pricing strategy**: What will we charge?
   - Cost structure and required margin
   - Customer value and willingness to pay
   - Competitor pricing and positioning
   - Initial vs. long-term pricing

4. **Plan sales and distribution**: How will customers access us?
   - Direct vs. indirect (resellers, partners, channels)
   - Geographic rollout (phased by region)
   - Channel conflicts and management
   - Sales model (enterprise, SMB, self-serve)

5. **Marketing and awareness plan**: How will we reach target customers?
   - Channels: Content, digital, events, PR, partnerships
   - Messaging: Key messages for each audience
   - Campaign calendar: Timing and sequencing
   - Budget allocation: Where to invest marketing dollars

6. **Sales support and enablement**: How will sales close deals?
   - Sales materials and collateral
   - Competitive positioning
   - Deal structure and discounting authority
   - Customer success plan

7. **Success metrics and KPIs**: How will we measure success?
   - Awareness metrics (reach, impressions, brand awareness surveys)
   - Lead generation (leads, cost per lead, lead quality)
   - Sales conversion (win rate, sales cycle length, deal size)
   - Revenue (quarterly targets, cohort analysis)
   - Customer health (churn, NPS, retention)
