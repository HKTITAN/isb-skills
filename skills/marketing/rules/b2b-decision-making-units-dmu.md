B2B purchases involve multiple roles:

**Initiators**: Recognize need or problem
- Often frontline employees
- May not have purchasing power but influence need recognition

**Users**: Will use the product
- Care about usability, features, support, ease
- Engineers evaluating software development tools

**Influencers**: Shape evaluation
- Technical experts, consultants, department heads
- Care about fit, quality, specifications
- IT directors evaluating security and compliance

**Decision-makers**: Approve purchase
- Executives, C-suite, procurement
- Care about budget, ROI, strategic fit
- Often most price-sensitive

**Buyers**: Execute purchase
- Procurement department, purchasing manager
- Care about terms, pricing, contracts, compliance

**Challenge**: One message won't satisfy all. Need multiple value propositions:
- For users: "Makes your job easier; saves you time"
- For technical influencers: "Integrates with your stack; meets compliance"
- For decision-makers: "Reduces costs by 30%; ROI in 6 months"
