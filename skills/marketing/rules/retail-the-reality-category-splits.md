- **Pure e-commerce wins**: Commodities where comparison and price matter
- **Pure physical wins**: Try-before-you-buy categories
- **Omnichannel wins**: Most mainstream categories where customers want flexibility

Example: Warby Parker—primarily DTC online but with physical try-on studios. Win with both channels.
