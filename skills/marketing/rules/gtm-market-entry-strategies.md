**Penetration**: Low price, wide distribution, heavy marketing to gain share quickly
- Risk: Price wars, race to bottom
- Best for: Competitive markets, price-sensitive customers

**Skimming**: High price, limited distribution, target early adopters
- Pros: Maximize short-term profit, build prestige
- Cons: Slower growth, vulnerable to competition
- Best for: Innovation, early mover advantage

**Differentiation**: Premium positioning, unique value, focused marketing to specific segments
- Pros: Defensible, builds loyalty, supports premium pricing
- Cons: Smaller market initially, requires sustained differentiation investment
- Best for: When you have genuine competitive advantage

**Blue Ocean**: Redefine category to create new uncontested market space
- Example: Cirque du Soleil redefined circus (removed animals/stars, added artistry/music/themes)
- Reduces direct competition but requires customer education
