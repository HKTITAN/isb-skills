**Intangibility**: Services are abstract, not physical
- Challenge: Can't touch/see/try before purchasing (high risk)
- Response: Use tangible cues (uniforms, environment), build reputation, offer guarantees/trials, educate

**Inseparability**: Production and consumption simultaneous; provider is part of experience
- Challenge: Quality depends on provider; hard to standardize
- Response: Employee training, hiring/retention, service recovery, self-service options, community building

**Variability**: Quality varies based on provider, time, context
- Challenge: Hard to ensure consistency
- Response: Service blueprinting, standardization where possible, quality assurance, flexibility, transparency

**Perishability**: Can't inventory; unsold capacity is lost forever
- Challenge: Empty seats, rooms, appointment slots generate zero revenue
- Response: Demand smoothing (off-peak pricing), pricing optimization, capacity planning, advance booking, overbooking
