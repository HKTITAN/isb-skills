Loyalty is the strongest measure of brand equity. It reduces churn, increases lifetime value, generates advocacy.

**Behavioral loyalty**: Repeat purchases, high frequency, large volumes (driven by habit, switching costs, lack of alternatives)

**Attitudinal loyalty**: Emotional commitment, preference, willingness to advocate (based on positive experiences, emotional connection, values alignment)

**Loyalty programs:**
- **Tier-based**: Higher tiers unlock greater rewards, encouraging increased spending
- **Point-based**: Accumulate points toward rewards or discounts
- **Value-based**: Connect brand values with member values
- **Exclusive access**: Special products, experiences, or sales for loyal customers
- **Referral programs**: Reward members for bringing new customers

**Building switching costs:**
- High-quality, consistent product/service delivery
- Exceptional customer service and relationship development
- Customization and personalization increasing customer fit
- Community and social ties among loyal customers
- Exclusive benefits unavailable elsewhere
- Integrated products and ecosystem lock-in

**Measuring loyalty:**
- Net Promoter Score (NPS): "How likely would you recommend?" (0-10 scale). NPS = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6). Ranges -100 to +100.
- Customer Retention Rate: % of customers in period t still active in period t+1
- Repeat Purchase Rate: % of customers making multiple purchases
- Customer Lifetime Value: Total profit attributed to relationship
- Share of Wallet: % of category spending going to your brand
- Brand Advocacy: Referral rates, reviews, word-of-mouth volume
