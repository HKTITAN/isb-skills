Send targeted messages directly to opted-in audience. Transactional (confirmations, shipping) or marketing (promotions, education).

When to use:
- High-intent audience (they opted in)
- Retention (staying top-of-mind with customers)
- High-value segments (email cheap per unit)
- Personalization (know customer history)

Economics:
- Extremely efficient: $0.01-0.10 per email
- ROI typically 4:1 or better
- Cost is mainly tools and list management
- CAC low if email is retention focused

Key metrics:
- Open rate: % who open (~20-30% good)
- CTR: % who click (~2-5% good)
- Conversion rate: % of clicks that become sales
- Unsubscribe rate: Keep below 0.5%
- List growth rate: New minus unsubscribes

Types:
- **Transactional**: Receipts, resets, status. High open rate (expected).
- **Promotional**: "Sale now!" Direct offer. Can feel spammy.
- **Newsletter**: Educational plus soft promotion. Builds loyalty.
- **Nurture sequences**: Educate, build trust, eventually sell. For long sales cycles.
- **Segmented campaigns**: Tailor to different segments.

Best practices:
- Build list organically (bought lists perform poorly)
- Segment by behavior
- Personalize beyond just name
- A/B test subject lines (huge impact on opens)
- Mobile optimize
- Make unsubscribe easy
