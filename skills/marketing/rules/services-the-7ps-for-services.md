Traditional 4Ps insufficient. Add:

**People**: Service quality depends entirely on employees
- Hiring for attitude and cultural fit
- Training (technical and emotional intelligence)
- Empowerment to solve problems
- Culture valuing service
- Recognition and rewards

**Physical Evidence**: Tangible environment shapes perception
- Facility design, cleanliness, ambiance
- Equipment, uniforms, signage
- Visible proof of quality (certifications, awards)

**Process**: Systems and procedures for delivery
- Efficiency, customization, reliability matter
- Transparency and ease-of-use
- Service blueprint mapping
- Quality controls at critical points
