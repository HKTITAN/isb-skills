## title: "Customer Journey: Awareness to Loyalty"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: awareness, customer, journey, loyalty, marketing, quantitative

**Awareness**: Customer recognizes need. Marketing creates visibility through ads, content, PR, word-of-mouth.

**Consideration**: Customer explores options. They need information to evaluate. Comparisons, reviews, case studies help.

**Decision**: Customer chooses and buys. Final friction should be removed—clear pricing, easy checkout, guarantees.

**Retention**: Post-purchase experience. Great product, support, engagement keep customers.

**Advocacy**: Satisfied customers recommend to others, becoming brand advocates.

Mapping your customer journey reveals where customers drop off—that's where to focus improvement.
