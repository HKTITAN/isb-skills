Measure brand health and perception:
- Brand awareness (aided, unaided, top-of-mind)
- Perceived quality, reliability, value
- Brand associations (what attributes does brand own?)
- Likelihood to recommend (NPS)
- Loyalty (repeat purchase, share of wallet)
- Emotional connection

Track over time to see brand trajectory. Use benchmark data to compare to competitors.
