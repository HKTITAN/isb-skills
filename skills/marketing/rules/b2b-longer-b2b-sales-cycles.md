Enterprise sales cycles often extend 6-12 months:

1. **Awareness/Education** (1-2 months): Learn about solution class
2. **Exploration** (2-3 months): Research vendors, request demos, get referrals
3. **Evaluation** (2-3 months): Detailed demos, pilots, reference calls, legal
4. **Approval** (1-2 months): Internal stakeholders approve, budget confirmed
5. **Negotiation** (1-2 months): Pricing, contract, implementation timeline
6. **Implementation** (ongoing): Onboarding, training, change management

Implications:
- Long nurture required; many touchpoints
- Content marketing critical (guides, webinars, case studies)
- Sales support needed; marketing and sales must align
- Customer success critical post-sale
