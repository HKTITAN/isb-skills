Hardest part of digital marketing is knowing which channel drove which sale.

**Attribution models:**
- **First-touch**: Credit first channel. Overvalues awareness.
- **Last-touch**: Credit last channel. Overvalues conversion channels. Most common but biased.
- **Linear**: Equal credit to all touchpoints
- **Time-decay**: More recent touchpoints get more credit
- **Position-based (U-shaped)**: First and last get 40%, middle 20%
- **Data-driven**: ML estimates credit based on historical conversion data

Key insight: Different models tell different stories. Use multiple and understand which tells most actionable story for your business.

**A/B Testing (Experimentation):**

Show version A to 50% of visitors and version B to 50%. Measure difference. Statistically significant result = causation.

Examples:
- Subject line testing ("Hurry!" vs. "Join 10,000+ customers")
- Landing page testing (video hero vs. text)
- Price testing ($99 vs. $109)

Statistical significance: Difference must be unlikely to happen by chance (typically 95% confidence level).

**Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO):**

Increase % of visitors who convert through testing:
- Headlines (benefit vs. curiosity)
- Value proposition (features vs. outcomes)
- Visual hierarchy
- CTA buttons (text, color, size, placement)
- Form length (shorter = higher submission, lower quality)
- Social proof (testimonials, logos, numbers)
- Friction (free trial vs. mandatory payment)

Micro-conversions (email signup, demo request, download) improve funnel stages before purchase.

Example: Cumulative testing improvements of 14% + 12% + 18% = 52% overall uplift from baseline.
