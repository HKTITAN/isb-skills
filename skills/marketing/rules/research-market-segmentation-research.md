Identify customer segments with different needs, behaviors, or value drivers. Methods:
- Demographics and psychographics (surveys)
- Behavioral data (purchase history, usage patterns)
- Needs-based segmentation (what problems are they trying to solve?)
- Value-based segmentation (willingness to pay by segment)

Evaluate segments:
- Size and growth potential
- Competitive intensity
- Strategic fit
- Profitability
- Barriers to entry
