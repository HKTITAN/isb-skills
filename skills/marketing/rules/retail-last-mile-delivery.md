**Models:**

- In-house: Brand control, high cost, requires scale
- Third-party carriers (FedEx, UPS): Scale, fixed cost, less control
- Specialized (DoorDash, Instacart): Same-day, flexible, higher cost
- Local partnerships: Faster, lower cost, partner dependent

**Speed expectations:**

- 2-hour: Premium, high cost, limited areas
- Same-day: Growing expectation for metros, 2-3x cost
- 2-3 day: Standard expectation
- 5-7 day: Acceptable for lower-price items

Free shipping sets expectations. Amazon Prime changed the game. Question: Does increased conversion justify margin hit?
