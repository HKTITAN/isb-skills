**SEO** is improving website visibility in organic (unpaid) search results. It encompasses technical optimization, content strategy, and link building.

Why SEO matters:
- Consumer behavior: 90%+ of customer journeys include a search
- Cost-effective: No per-click cost (unlike paid advertising)
- Credibility: Top rankings signal authority and trustworthiness
- Long-term value: Content and authority accumulate; paid ads stop when spending stops
- Intent-driven: Searchers have explicit intent (problem-solving, comparison, purchase)

**SEO Fundamentals:**

On-page SEO:
- Keyword research: Identify search terms customers use (search volume, difficulty, intent)
- Content optimization: Create quality, deep, relevant content targeting keywords
- Title tags and meta descriptions: Craft compelling titles for search results
- Header structure: Organize with H1, H2, H3 hierarchy
- Internal linking: Link related content; establish hierarchy and distribute authority
- URL structure: Clear, keyword-inclusive URLs

Technical SEO:
- Site speed: Fast page load improves rankings and UX
- Mobile optimization: Google prioritizes mobile-friendly sites
- Crawlability: Search engine bots can access and understand content
- XML sitemaps and robots.txt: Help bots discover content
- Structured data (schema markup): Semantic HTML helps understanding

Off-page SEO:
- Link building: Earn links from high-authority sites (signals credibility)
- Brand mentions: References to brand (with or without links) improve rankings
- Local citations: Directory mentions important for local search

**Keyword research strategy:**

Search intent drives keyword selection:
- Informational intent ("How does SEO work?"): Create guides, educational content
- Navigational intent ("Facebook login"): Ensure official pages appear
- Commercial intent ("Best project management software"): Comparison guides, reviews
- Transactional intent ("Buy laptop"): Product pages, checkout optimization

Content strategy: Create content pillars (major theme clusters) where you develop deep expertise. Within each pillar, create cluster of related content (pillar article + sub-articles + visuals + interactive).

Example for HR SaaS:
- Pillar: Employee engagement and retention
- Cluster: "Complete Guide to Employee Engagement" (pillar) + "50 Engagement Ideas" + "Measuring Engagement" + "Remote Team Engagement"

Benefits:
- Topical authority signals expertise
- Cross-linking distributes authority
- Captures long-tail keyword variations
- Supports customer journey (awareness → consideration → purchase)
