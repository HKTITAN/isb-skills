## title: "Financial Metrics"
impact: HIGH
impactDescription: "Important concept frequently tested in cases"
tags: finance, financial, marketing, metrics, quantitative

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):**
CAC = Total Marketing & Sales Spend / Number of Customers Acquired

Example: Spent $100K on marketing, acquired 50 customers → CAC = $2,000/customer

**Customer Lifetime Value (CLV):**
CLV = (Average Order Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan) - CAC

Or: CLV = (Average Revenue per Customer - Average Cost per Customer) × Average Customer Lifespan

Example: $50 average order × 24 times/year × 3 year lifespan - $1,200 CAC = $2,400 lifetime value

**Payback Period:**
Payback Period = CAC / Average Monthly Profit per Customer

Healthy payback is <12 months for SaaS, <6 months for e-commerce

**LTV:CAC Ratio:**
LTV:CAC = Lifetime Value / Customer Acquisition Cost

Healthy ratio is 3:1 or better (each $1 spent on acquisition generates $3+ lifetime profit)

**Retention Rate:**
Retention Rate = (Customers at period end - New customers) / Customers at period start

Example: 1,000 customers start January, gain 100, have 1,050 at end = (1,050-100)/1,000 = 95% retention

**Churn Rate:**
Churn = (Customers lost in period / Customers at start of period) × 100

5% monthly churn = 60% annual churn (customers decline throughout year)
