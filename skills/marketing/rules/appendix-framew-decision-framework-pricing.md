Choose pricing approach:

1. **Cost-plus**: When value = cost (commodities), cost transparency required
2. **Competitive**: When products similar, price is decision factor
3. **Value-based**: When value varies, you can prove value, willing to pay differs by segment

Value-based yields highest margins; cost-plus ensures profitability; competitive matches market
