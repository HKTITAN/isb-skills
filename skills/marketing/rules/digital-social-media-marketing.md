Create and share content on platforms (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter) where audience spends time.

When to use:
- B2C brands with broad appeal
- Thought leadership (executives sharing insights)
- Community building (brand advocates, loyal customers)
- Viral potential (emotionally resonant content)

Economics:
- Organic reach declining; platforms prioritize paid
- Paid social can be efficient with good targeting
- CAC varies: $1-5 for B2C, $100+ for B2B
- Requires consistent content creation

Key metrics:
- Reach: How many people saw content?
- Engagement: Likes, comments, shares (resonance)
- CTR: % who click
- Conversion: % of clicks that become sales

Best practices:
- Post when audience is active
- Use video (higher engagement)
- Encourage comments and respond
- Consistent branding and voice
- A/B test copy, images, timing
