Choose segmentation approach:

1. **Geographic** or **Demographic**: External characteristics
2. **Psychographic**: Values and lifestyle
3. **Behavioral**: Actual usage and purchase patterns
4. **Needs-based**: Underlying problems customers solve

Needs-based most actionable for marketing strategy
