Omnichannel means treating online and physical as one ecosystem:

**BOPIS** (Buy Online, Pickup In Store):
- Customer orders online
- Picks up at nearest store within hours
- Reduces shipping costs, accelerates delivery, drives store traffic

**Buy Online, Return In Store:**
- Easier than shipping back
- Builds confidence in online purchasing

**Unified Inventory:**
- Inventory isn't "online" and "store"—it's all one
- Customers see if product in nearby stores
- If out of stock, can order for delivery
- Challenges: Real-time accuracy, cannibalization

**In-Store Mobile Experience:**
- QR codes linking to online pages
- Scan items for price, reviews, availability
- Mobile checkout reduces friction

**Staff as Channel:**
- Associates have tablets for inventory lookup, ordering, questions
- Create frictionless experiences
