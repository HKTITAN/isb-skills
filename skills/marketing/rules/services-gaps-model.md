Service quality gaps:
1. **Knowledge gap**: Management doesn't understand customer expectations
2. **Standards gap**: Doesn't translate expectations into standards
3. **Performance gap**: Staff don't meet standards
4. **Communication gap**: Marketing promises exceed delivery
5. **Perceived service gap**: Customer perception ≠ actual service

Managing all five gaps ensures quality.
