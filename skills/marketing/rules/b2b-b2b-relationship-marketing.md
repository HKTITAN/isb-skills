## title: "B2B Relationship Marketing"
impact: MEDIUM
impactDescription: "Supporting concept that enhances domain understanding"
tags: marketing, relationship, technology

Success depends on trust and relationships:

Account-based marketing (ABM): Focus on target accounts with personalized campaigns

Executive engagement: C-level relationships, executive briefings, advisory boards

Thought leadership: Executives publish insights; position company as expert

Industry events: Conferences, networking, sponsorships

Customer advisory boards: Involve key customers in product roadmap

Value of relationships:

- Switching costs: Deep relationships reduce switching
- Expansion: Trusted vendor gets first look at new projects
- Price resilience: Strong relationships support premium pricing
- Referrals and advocacy: Satisfied customers recommend
