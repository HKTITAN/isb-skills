To develop a pricing strategy:

1. **Calculate cost structure**: What's the floor price where economics work?
2. **Understand customer value**: What value does offering create? What are customers currently paying for similar value?
3. **Identify competitive set**: What are relevant alternatives? How do they price? What's your advantage?
4. **Make positioning choice**: Will you compete on price, value, or prestige? This determines pricing tier.
5. **Set initial price**: Using cost floor, value ceiling, and competitive midpoint, set hypothesis
6. **Test and refine**: Monitor demand, profit margin, customer feedback; adjust if needed
7. **Manage channels**: Ensure consistent pricing across channels to avoid conflict
8. **Monitor competitors**: Revisit pricing regularly; adjust if market conditions change

Key principle: You can always lower prices. Raising prices is much harder. Better to underprice initially and raise gradually than overprice and destroy demand.
