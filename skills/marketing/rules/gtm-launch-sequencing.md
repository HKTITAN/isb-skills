**Phase 1: Build awareness and interest**
- PR and thought leadership
- Content marketing (blog, guides, webinars)
- Paid advertising (depending on budget)
- Early adopter outreach

**Phase 2: Drive trial and evaluation**
- Free trial or freemium access
- Demos and webinars
- Case studies and social proof
- Community engagement

**Phase 3: Scale and expansion**
- Increase marketing investment
- Optimize channels and messaging
- Build partner ecosystem
- Upsell and expand existing customers

**Phase 4: Mature and optimize**
- Reduce customer acquisition costs through referrals and organic
- Focus on retention and expansion
- Optimize pricing and positioning
