High-impact channels:

- **Content marketing**: Blogs, whitepapers, guides, webinars (builds authority, educates across sales cycle)
- **Email**: Nurture leads, share insights, drive events
- **LinkedIn**: Target executives, thought leadership, ads
- **Industry events**: Conferences, trade shows, networking
- **Sales development**: Outbound prospecting, relationship building
- **Customer success**: Post-sale support, retention, expansion
- **PR and analyst relations**: Gartner, Forrester; credibility, positioning
- **Paid search**: Target high-intent prospects
