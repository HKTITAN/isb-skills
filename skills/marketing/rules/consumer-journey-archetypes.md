Not all customers follow the same pattern. Understanding archetypes helps tailor strategy:

**Routine Purchase**: Low-involvement, habitual decisions
- Focus: Make purchasing convenient, build habit, reduce switching friction
- Examples: Grocery staples, regular coffee orders, routine medications

**Joyride**: Impulsive, low-consideration purchases based on emotional appeal
- Focus: Emotional appeal, visibility, impulse triggers
- Examples: Trending fashion, impulse candy at checkout, last-minute entertainment

**Trek**: Purposeful, moderate-involvement decisions
- Focus: Detailed information, credible expert, comparisons
- Examples: Buying a camera, choosing a hotel, selecting a computer

**Odyssey**: Complex, high-involvement decisions over extended time
- Focus: Relationship building, education, trust establishment
- Examples: House purchase, college selection, long-term service providers
