**Building equity:**
1. Clear, consistent positioning: Know what you stand for; communicate repeatedly across touchpoints
2. Performance excellence: Deliver on brand promise; under-promise and over-deliver
3. Emotional connection: Tell brand stories that resonate; create community
4. Integrated marketing: Align all touchpoints (product, pricing, distribution, promotion) with positioning
5. Innovation: Evolve to stay relevant; don't become dated or irrelevant

**Protecting equity:**
- Maintain quality; never compromise to save costs
- Extend into logical categories; avoid stretch extensions (luxury brand opening discount line is risky)
- Manage brand associations carefully; distance yourself from negative associations
- Defend reputation; respond to crises quickly and authentically
- Invest continuously; brands fade without ongoing investment
