Systematic analysis of competitors:
- Product features, pricing, positioning, distribution
- Marketing messages and strategies
- Customer reviews and sentiment
- Financial performance and growth
- Strategic moves and investments
- Market share and momentum

Methods:
- SEC filings and financial analysis
- Customer review sites (ratings, commentary)
- Mystery shopping
- Competitor website and content analysis
- Industry reports and analyst coverage
