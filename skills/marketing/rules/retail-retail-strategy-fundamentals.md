**Store Format Decisions:**
- Full-line retailer (Walmart, Target): Everything, low price, high volume
- Specialty retailer (Apple, Lululemon): Deep expertise, premium, high margin
- Discount retailer (T.J. Maxx, Costco): Reduced selection, low price, volume
- Pop-up/temporary: Short-term, lower cost, build buzz

**Location Selection:**
- High-traffic areas cost more but drive footfall
- Secondary locations cost less but require brand awareness/loyalty
- Urban vs. suburban vs. rural have different profiles and competition

**Store Productivity Metrics:**
- Sales per square foot: Excellent = $1K+, struggling = $200
- Traffic: Customer visits
- Conversion rate: % of visitors who buy
- Basket size: Spending per trip
