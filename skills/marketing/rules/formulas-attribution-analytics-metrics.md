**Multi-touch attribution**: Distribute credit across touchpoints, not just first or last

**A/B test significance**: Typically 95% confidence level required (difference unlikely to happen by chance)

**Funnel analysis**: Track drop-off rates at each stage (awareness → interest → consideration → conversion)

**Cohort analysis**: Group customers by acquisition period/channel, track retention/LTV over time

**Customer journey analysis**: Track how customers move across channels before converting
