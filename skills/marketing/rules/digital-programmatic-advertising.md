Use software to automatically buy/optimize digital ads (display, video, mobile) across many sites and apps.

When to use:
- Scale needed
- Retargeting (very efficient)
- Broad awareness
- Performance optimization

Economics:
- CPM (cost per thousand impressions): $5-$30
- CPC (cost per click): $0.50-$5
- Auction-based bidding
- Requires budget (hard to test small)

Key metrics:
- Impressions: Times ad shown
- CTR: % of impressions that click
- Conversion rate: % of clicks that convert
- ROAS: Revenue ÷ spend
- CAC: Cost per acquisition

Best practices:
- Use retargeting (often 2-3x more efficient)
- Test different audiences
- Use dynamic ads (show viewed products)
- Optimize for conversion, not clicks
- Monitor for ad fraud
