**Quantitative (Numerical):**
- Surveys: Large sample, statistical power, representative
- Conjoint analysis: Measure product feature preferences and price trade-offs
- A/B testing: Randomized experiments proving causation
- Observational: Track actual behavior (purchase patterns, website behavior)
- Pricing research: Van Westendorp (price sensitivity), Gabor-Granger (purchase likelihood)

**Qualitative (Exploratory):**
- Focus groups: Group discussion revealing attitudes, needs, decision drivers
- Depth interviews: One-on-one detailed exploration
- Ethnography: Observe customers in natural environment
- Case studies: In-depth examination of specific customers/segments
- Social listening: Analyze online conversation, sentiment, unmet needs

**Best Practice:**
Start with qualitative to understand the landscape. Then use quantitative to validate and measure at scale.
