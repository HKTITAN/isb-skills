Pay commission to affiliates for each customer referred. Affiliates include special link; you track sales.

When to use:
- Healthy margins (affiliate takes 10-50%)
- Broad distribution needed
- Performance-based cost appealing
- Word-of-mouth potential

Economics:
- No upfront cost, only commission on sales
- Commission: 10-50% depending on industry
- Lower net cost if affiliates productive
- Requires recruitment and management

Best practices:
- Recruit affiliates with aligned audiences
- Provide marketing materials
- Track performance; incentivize top performers
- Monitor for fraud
- Build relationships with high performers
