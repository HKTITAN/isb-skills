Attitudes (favorable/unfavorable evaluations) are formed through:
1. Direct experience (using product forms attitudes)
2. Social influence (friends' opinions matter)
3. Media and marketing (consistent communication builds attitudes)
4. Belief updating (new information changes attitudes)

Changing negative attitudes is hard because:
- Confirmation bias: Seek confirming information, ignore disconfirming
- Belief persistence: Strongly held beliefs resist change even with evidence

Prevention is easier than repair. Don't let negative attitudes form. If they do, change requires sustained positive experiences and time.
