| Dimension | B2B | B2C |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Decision-maker | Multiple stakeholders | Individual or household |
| Decision process | Rational, ROI-focused, formal | Mix of rational and emotional |
| Sales cycle | Months/years | Days/weeks |
| Purchase frequency | Lower frequency, larger values | Higher frequency, smaller values |
| Relationship | Long-term partnerships | Transactional |
| Marketing | Relationship, thought leadership | Brand, emotion, convenience |
